- `panel.json`: exact checkpoint/config/data/native bs4 group, original/treated literal histories, candidate token sequences, offsets, source hashes and finite budgets.
- `source-snapshots/unit.md`, `source-snapshots/owner_recurrence_onset.py`: frozen protocol and executed instrumentation. `run.sh` and per-mode logs/PIDs/exits retain actual launch.
- `review/admission.json`, `review/native-rows.json`, `review/full-image.png`, `review/native-rows1-8.png`, `review/contrast-full.png`, `review/contrast-local.png`: bounded physical evidence; original rows3/4 HOLD,5/6 confirmed same dominant owner, neighboring unvisited B. No exhaustive scene census.
- `annotation-identity.json`: current export versus accepted snapshot/image byte checks;63 positives, absent ignore semantics. Original frozen banks remain unchanged.
- `runtime/native/raw.json`: exact complete original four-member batch tokens/text/stops. `capture.pt`: full logits, actual final head inputs, MRoPE positions and literal history for target action19..69.
- `runtime/scores/{possible_seed,possible_recurrence,confirmed_seed,confirmed_recurrence,normal_transition}.pt`: all candidate row-token plus next-boundary full-vocabulary logits, head inputs, positions, tokens and history. Later candidate prefixes differ after first fork. Normal-transition target history comes from the saved equal-norm route, with original companions, scored under original readout.
- `runtime/{native,scores}/readout.pt`: actual effective base+delta coordinate output rows, input rows,1000 token IDs, original FP64 norms/factors and absent bias. Receipt binds loaded model, native input identity, source, parameter invariance and cost.
- `reduce.py` → `reduction.json`: offline fixed-h norm/alignment scores, candidate complete-row likelihoods, first forks, invalid geometry mass, native/full parity and old17/current63 match ledgers.
- `verify.py` → `independent-verification.json`: separate likelihood reconstruction, token corruption falsification, exact raw replay, current-bank scoring and source hashes.
- `saved-current-scoring.json`: CPU rescoring of existing original/normalized full trajectories, not new treatment runs.
- `failed-attempt-0/`, `mechanical-source-repair.json`, `source-snapshots/native-{predecessor,current}.py`: zero-forward stale whole-file binding failure; executed prepare/replay functions are AST-identical, only unused helpers added. Original failure and panel preserved.
- `result.json`, `terminal.json`, `artifact-bindings.json`: candidate conclusion, costs, process closure, immutable evidence index. Root acceptance remains separate.

Not captured: layer/attention/query/key/value archives, new free repair continuations, new sampling, training gradients or exhaustive physical negatives. Full coordinate logits and full-vocabulary vectors are retained at every selected candidate position, not only plotted summaries.
