# Discovery and route value: an available owner is not yet a useful policy

**Question:** is the shortage in trustworthy support, useful complete continuations, or realization by the student? The same image, exact prefix and remaining budget define a candidate's context; a candidate from another history does not automatically transfer its certificate.

## Evidence chain

[Fixed-prefix sampling](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15/investigations/qwen3-vl-dense-enumeration/experiments/2026-07-14-sampled-rescue-object-transition-causal-replay/results.md) showed multiple object modes locally. [Trajectory-versus-union analysis](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15/investigations/qwen3-vl-dense-enumeration/experiments/2026-07-21-individual-trajectory-versus-union-support-audit/results.md) distinguished good complete sampled outputs from complementary partial owner sets. Their union is evidence of support, not one joint executable route.

The later [natural opportunity census](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15/investigations/qwen3-vl-dense-enumeration/experiments/2026-09-09-natural-candidate-opportunity/results.md) found beneficial complete outputs in the same-checkpoint K4 bank. The [round-one greedy read](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15/investigations/qwen3-vl-dense-enumeration/experiments/2026-09-09-round1-greedy-realization/results.md) did not realize the registered sample-gained owners. Existence and student learning must therefore be measured independently; a sampling gain alone cannot certify a compiler.

[Two owner-row continuation cases](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15/investigations/qwen3-vl-dense-enumeration/experiments/2026-09-10-owner-row-continuation-robustness/results.md) showed that supplied partial or full target rows could lead to useful free successors and retained old owners. [Recursive composition admission](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15/investigations/qwen3-vl-dense-enumeration/experiments/2026-09-11-recursive-owner-composition/results.md) found many conditional closures, but owner/class/extent ambiguity limited automatic learning-label promotion. [Residual composition](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15/investigations/qwen3-vl-dense-enumeration/experiments/2026-09-12-parallel-owner-research/composition/residual-results.md) later lost existing owners. An immediate good `c + w` is not proof that the whole future remains good.

## Candidate bookkeeping that matters

Keep three identities distinct: the physical owner, its literal row, and the exact conditioning history. Multiple rows can represent one owner; a literal coordinate change can be harmless; one target can be useful after one history and harmful after another. Preserve remaining token budget and full consequence policy as part of a witness.

Unknown spans may remain as literal conditions without direct positive supervision. Removing them changes the state, so the old suffix evidence no longer certifies the new route. Conversely, requiring every candidate already be greedy or every full route already be perfect would exclude precisely the repair obligations the training-first task is meant to learn.

## Reopening conditions

Before increasing K or enlarging acquisition, show that lack of support rather than poor compilation is the bottleneck on the declared population. [The K4/K8 study](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15/investigations/qwen3-vl-dense-enumeration/experiments/2026-08-22-human13-owner-credit-nk-factorial/results.md) does not establish a monotonic benefit of more samples. Its repetition-penalty conditions must not be mixed.

Before preferring a branch, specify whether its consequence is under the current student, a frozen model, sampling or finite correction. These are different values, not interchangeable estimators of one future. Match support, verification cost and old-owner loss when comparing candidates.
