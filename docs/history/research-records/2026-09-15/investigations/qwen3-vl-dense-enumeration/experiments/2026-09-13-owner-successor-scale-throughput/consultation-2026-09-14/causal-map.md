## Bottom line

**High confidence:** useful conditional routes exist, and some can be compiled into unpatched natural greedy behavior. **High confidence:** neither entry learning, one protected successor, nor suppressing strict repeats guarantees a finite useful enumeration. **Moderate confidence:** the present research bottleneck is distinguishing *which continuation obligations fail under which states*, not choosing between a universal exposure-bias story and a universal owner-ledger story.

I would put a small **N16/A endpoint × literal-history crossover ahead of another training comparison**. It directly asks whether the current common-learning regression is mediated sufficiently by changed text paths or persists under old useful paths. Historical crossovers already demonstrate both types; extrapolating either to current A is unwarranted. B is not necessary for this first contrast because A already produces most of the shortening/repeat suppression and substantial owner loss.

Root freshly reports no recoverable A/B intermediate adapters or optimizer snapshots. Therefore Pro's retrospective dose readout is unavailable. Do not retrain to manufacture that plot; register a few saved states in the next independently justified fit.

## Causal structure and identifiability

For a fixed image/prompt and remaining budget:

```text
training recipe -> parameters theta -> conditional token preferences
                               |                    |
                               v                    v
emitted text history -------> computed KV -------> next tokens -> next history
                               ^                    |
                        experimental patch          +-> EOS / row admission
                                                    +-> category / geometry / syntax
                                                                    |
                                                                    v
                                                    measured TP / physical presence / repeats
```

The arrows describe computational dependence, not an identified internal semantic variable. In ordinary deterministic execution, fixed parameters and exact history determine normal KV. A patch adds state not fixed by text; its influence can propagate into subsequently computed KV even after patch operations stop. Training changes both earlier choices and the continuation map at an identical prefix. Measurement can change without physical presence changing.

## Specific challenges to Pro

- Its dose fallback is sensible, but the actual checkpoint inventory removes retrospective dose from this round. Future saved states are needed, not new training solely to recover history.
- F/R is a policy intervention on a **supply-gated subset**, not a clean unconditional exposure-bias treatment. Fix the original package denominator, report refreshed/eligible/failed/unknown counts, and use intent-to-treat across all original tasks. Zero refresh is no treatment contrast, not a null history result.
- Full-suffix admission followed by training only c plus old protection can still destroy an admitted suffix at fixed h+c. It removes a bad-teacher explanation but does not supply a preservation guarantee. If current harm is recipient-dominant, this is a substantive gap in the chosen next axis.
- Keeping old w protection at its original histories is legitimate; silently moving its teacher distribution to refreshed h' is not. Revalidated new witnesses alter the policy's data support and should be reported as part of that compound gated policy.
- Full-row literal margins guarantee only tokenwise realization under the exact certified prefix and deterministic execution. They do not guarantee root visitation, downstream owner retention, or preference among all semantically valid row renderings. First-fork pairwise margin is weaker still: another token can beat both compared alternatives.
- The existing pool already has distinct target strings at identical conditions. It is invalid to demand all become greedy simultaneously or treat every alternative string as a wrong owner. A shared-prefix tree can preserve alternatives, but its leaves must retain their own validated successor obligations rather than mixing suffixes across branches. This is an acceptance/target-meaning issue before it is an algorithm choice.

### What matched evidence changes the next action?

**External labels first** is justified operationally if either:

- With *identical frozen outputs*, census-backed scoring materially reverses the decision or removes the apparent owner-loss conclusion; then repair the evaluation overlay before optimizing against the old metric. This does not by itself authorize more training labels.
- The frozen physical opportunity set shows label-absent owners dominate otherwise admissible missed targets, and a bounded matched supply comparison shows that adding only their reviewed label overlay exposes useful full-suffix witnesses at the **same checkpoint, image, history, nomination limits and generation budget**. Compare old-GT eligibility with old-GT-plus-overlay eligibility; keep newly annotated but unusable branches in the denominator. Then external completion addresses an observed supply bottleneck rather than merely increasing box counts. Root must freeze what “dominates” means before exposing these outcomes; the result is panel-local, not proof of historical SFT causation.

**Algorithm first** is justified if census-backed matching preserves the current unfavorable tradeoff and substantial failures concern already-known, clearly resolved owners; or new physical labels increase possible targets but those targets still lack safe continuation/greedy realization. In that case, carry only the small accepted positive/unknown overlay forward, and return to the bounded path-versus-recipient/conditional-realization test instead of expanding annotation. This does not prohibit future label improvements.

If the user means **causal training-label completeness** by “main cause,” neither rule above proves it. The minimum later causal test is a separate paired training contract from the same initialization on the same images: original versus reviewed completed supervision, with algorithm, optimizer, images and an explicit matched exposure/loss-mass policy held fixed; evaluate on an independently censused panel. Adding missing rows necessarily changes positive token mass unless matched deliberately, so simply training longer on a larger completed set is not a clean missing-label effect. Do not launch this ablation until the cheap census/supply evidence makes it decision-bearing.

**Finite stop:** eight images, one unaided census and one capped resolution pass; optional one external-detector proposal pass; one paired rescore. If supplied-history acquisition becomes necessary, propose a separate cap of at most two newly reviewed owner opportunities per image and one old/new eligibility comparison, with no backfill. Report inconclusive if unresolved objects can reverse the action. Do not expand until a preferred conclusion emerges, and do not convert this into a comprehensive annotation project.
