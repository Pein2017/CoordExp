## Interpretation and next decision

**Supported inference:** local credible branch supervision can become natural
whole-rollout improvement and transfer beyond fitted images; it is no longer
only a teacher-forced capability. But learning an owner entry does not by
itself establish future exclusion or preserve every incumbent. KL protects
registered conditional distributions, not the complete reachable owner set.

**Strongest alternatives:** improvements could combine better path access,
termination and task-specific regularization rather than a general owner
accounting mechanism. The new cup loop also fits ordinary class/coordinate
overgeneralization. Neither special-token storage nor a hidden ledger has
been identified causally. The acquisition yield is a real bottleneck:
machine-compatible continuations are substantially more common than trusted
physical transitions, and11 images remain a narrow training population.

**Next proposal, not launched:** use the completed candidate as a research
anchor, not a default replacement. Compare matched positive-only continuation
against the same positives plus active later-row negative learning from real
greedyIoU>0.95 repeats. Bind affected loss positions and event counts; evaluate
new-loop incidence, below-threshold drift, owner gains/losses and valid
unlabeled preservation at the same3084 budget. The cup is a required
counterexample, not the sole tuning image. Fewer exact duplicate boxes with
more jitter, hallucination or lost real owners is failure, not success.
One validated real-prefix refresh is a separate subsequent factor rather
than bundled with the negative objective. No new architecture or broad matcher
rewrite is required to test this narrower question.
