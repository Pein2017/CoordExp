# Untouched Natural-Terminal Boundary Statistical Analysis

Executed evidence and the bounded judgment are owned by
[the completed result](results.md).

## Decision and Outcome

This read-only unit decides whether the existing 200-boundary natural-stop
panel is statistically understood well enough to design and interpret a later
forced-opener release study. The owning outcome is the distribution of
diagnostic continuation evidence and panel composition, not released owner
recovery and not final free-rollout owner-set coverage.

The original task remains one `list all objects` prompt and one free
autoregressive completion. No inference, forced token, optimizer update,
training, prompt change, or architecture change is authorized in this unit.

## Fixed Evidence Population

The population is exactly the 200
`untouched_terminal_with_remaining_owner` boundaries in the immutable manifest
from the completed continuation-locality unit. Every record:

- is a distinct image absent from the 1,440-image training event bank;
- is a Source rollout that naturally emitted the terminal token after a
  complete row;
- has at least one verified trusted physical owner still uncovered according
  to the frozen census; and
- has identical image, prompt, literal prefix, and FP32 scoring conditions
  across Source, transition step 36, pairwise step 90, and owner-conditioned
  step 90.

The panel is a deterministic diagnostic sample, not a random population
sample. Its materializer sorts eligible boundaries by prefix depth and then a
stable hash before taking 200. Statistics therefore describe this frozen
panel and must not be promoted to all model stops without a sampling argument.

## Falsifiable Questions

1. How strong are the original Source stops, and how many are near the
   row-opener-versus-terminal decision boundary?
2. How many boundaries move from Source's nonpositive diagnostic margin to a
   positive margin under each trained checkpoint, and how robust is that count
   to requiring margins greater than `+0.5` or `+1.0`?
3. Are the changes broad across prefix depth, scene density, and remaining-
   owner count, or driven by one narrow stratum or a few outliers?
4. Do checkpoints move the same boundaries, or do their positive-margin sets
   and paired deltas identify materially different states?
5. What owner-category and remaining-owner composition must a later release
   study preserve when reporting intended recovery, another uncovered owner,
   repeats, invalid rows, and unmatched geometry?

The strongest alternative to a broad continuation effect is threshold
selection: a small number of shallow or already-near-terminal states could
produce the aggregate mean while most actual stops remain unchanged. Paired
threshold counts, strata, correlations, and influence summaries separate
these explanations without model execution.

## Primary Estimands

For each checkpoint, compute on the same 200 boundaries:

1. the distribution of
   `log P(canonical new-row opener) - log P(terminal token)`;
2. checkpoint-minus-Source paired changes with a deterministic bootstrap
   interval for the mean;
3. positive diagnostic-margin counts at thresholds `0`, `+0.5`, and `+1.0`;
4. Source-nonpositive to checkpoint-positive crossings and the reverse;
5. descriptive Wilson intervals for binary fractions;
6. stratified counts by prefix-depth bin, object-count band, and verified
   remaining-owner-count bin;
7. Spearman rank correlations between margin changes and Source margin,
   prefix depth, object count, and remaining-owner count; and
8. overlap and nesting of positive-margin boundary sets across checkpoints.

Category frequencies count verified uncovered physical owners, not images.
Per-image fields remain available in a compact case ledger for later release
analysis.

## Meaning-Bearing Invariants

- `natural terminal` is the observed historical Source greedy action and is
  not redefined by this analysis.
- A positive diagnostic margin means only that the canonical row opener
  outranks the terminal token in the declared FP32 two-token comparison. It
  does not prove that free decoding would emit the opener, because other
  tokens, numerical precision, and generation-time processing remain outside
  that comparison.
- A checkpoint crossing from nonpositive to positive is therefore a
  **two-token margin crossing**, not a decode flip or an owner recovery.
- Remaining-owner identity and category are read from the same trusted census
  and candidate annotation used by the frozen manifest. Ambiguous or
  untrusted owners are not silently added.
- Statistical intervals describe within-panel resampling or binomial
  uncertainty summaries; the panel's deterministic selection prevents a
  population-generalization claim.

## Minimal Execution Path and Artifact

1. Validate all eight locality receipts against the immutable manifest and
   verify the same 200 boundary IDs for every checkpoint.
2. Materialize one 200-row analysis ledger by joining manifest metadata,
   trusted remaining-owner categories, and checkpoint margins.
3. Compute aggregate, paired, threshold, overlap, stratified, correlation, and
   influence summaries.
4. Inspect diagnostic inconsistencies, near-threshold cases, and representative
   high- and low-margin strata.
5. Write a bounded result and stop for user discussion before any release.
