## Findings and required narrowing

### K10: post-opener conditional oracle-routing sufficiency

The A3 `gt:2299:2` K00 endpoint matches covered owner
`gt:2299:0`; K10 matches strict target owner `gt:2299:2` with a valid native
row and no unmatched flag. This is real hard target-region routing
sufficiency at the tested interface.

It is not a natural-admission result. The exact H0 boundary ends with the
previous committed row, while the K00/K10 generated suffixes contain nine
tokens and omit the leading `<|object_ref_start|>` token. The endpoint row
receipt includes that opener as token `151646`, so the old free-row calls were
seeded after the opener. The old `natural=true` field does not distinguish this
condition: it predates the required `admission_mode` and
`opener_generated_by_model` cross-field checks. Thus the supported claim is
`post_opener_conditional_oracle_routing_sufficiency`, not spontaneous row
admission, a natural owner slot, or a STOP control.

The new unit must reproduce K10 from a pre-opener natural boundary. A seeded
K00/K01/K10/K12 comparison may be retained only as a separately labeled
conditional diagnostic and cannot satisfy the S natural threshold.

### D10 and D20: pre-opener row-entry grammar plus seeded conditional identity null

The old scalar pre-opener diagnostics are decision-relevant, but they are not
rollout endpoints. On the same A3 boundary, native D00 gives opener log
probability `-0.0002530493`, opener-versus-best margin `+10.1791255`, and
opener-minus-native-terminal `+15.7949629`. D10 changes these to
`-6.8128872`, `-1.5919433`, and `+4.4982185`; its best competing token is a
coordinate token (`152195`). D20 changes them to `-2.3558536`, `+2.5938082`,
and `+7.7663331`; its best competing token is
`<|object_ref_end|>` (`151647`). These are row-entry grammar effects.

After an opener is supplied, D10 and D20 both produce the same conditional
physical owner as D00 at one row (`gt:2299:0`) and the same three-row owner
bookkeeping (`G={0,3,4}`, `L={1}`, `net=+2`). The opener was supplied before
each released row, including after completed rows. This is a seeded
conditional identity null for the tested history handles; it does not show
that history is idle at the natural admission boundary, does not identify
STOP control, and does not establish uncovered-owner reallocation.

The new unit therefore separates `pre_opener_natural` from
`post_opener_seeded` and measures admission, STOP, identity, and geometry as
different outcomes.

The A3 Y10 and Y11 rows are parser- and native-wrapper-valid, but their first

The arithmetic `Delta_dynamic=0`, `Delta_static=-1`, `tau=0` is therefore only
