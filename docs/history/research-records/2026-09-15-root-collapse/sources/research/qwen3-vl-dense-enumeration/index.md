## Choose a reading budget

**Fast catch-up:** current → current experiment's state and result. Recover the objective, latest user boundary, evaluation semantics and unfinished obligation. Do not read every past experiment.

**Research catch-up:** current → [story](story.md) → one or two question pages below → their decisive source results. Consult [vocabulary](vocabulary.md) when a checkpoint or metric name is ambiguous.

| Question | Read for |
|---|---|
| [Capacity and readout](questions/capacity-and-readout.md) | What finite fitting establishes, and why it is not transfer |
| [Discovery and route value](questions/discovery-and-route-value.md) | Whether a candidate exists and whether taking it has useful complete consequences |
| [Greedy compilation](questions/greedy-compilation.md) | Conditional fit, route access and the current cumulative training task |

## Retrieval rather than compulsory reading

[Ideas and reopening conditions](ideas.md) keeps unfinished alternatives visible. [experiments.jsonl](experiments.jsonl) inventories all imported experiment roots and nested protocols, with question tags and exact source paths. It is a lookup index, not a live-job list or an automatically validated scientific verdict.

Older foundational work remains in [ideas](../ideas/index.md), [decisions](../decisions/index.md) and the [historical knowledge capsules](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15/investigations/coordexp-experiment-knowledge-handoff/index.md). The [archive guide](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15/README.md) explains original-path references and preserved identities.

There is no new `investigations` category. The old path is only a compatibility alias for historical evidence and existing data consumers. New work belongs in this program, not through that alias.
