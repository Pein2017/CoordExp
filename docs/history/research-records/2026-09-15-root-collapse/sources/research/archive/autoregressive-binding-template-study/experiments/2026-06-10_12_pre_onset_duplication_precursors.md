# June 10-12 Pre-Onset Duplication Precursors

This summary absorbs 19 raw records about pre-onset coordinate-basin drift and route/content precursors before a duplicate or wrong coordinate is emitted.

## Read

The records argue that duplication can be visible before the emitted duplicate row, through coordinate-basin drift, history features, residual asymmetry, and Layer 16 route/content signals. This makes pre-onset state a useful diagnostic surface rather than only post-hoc emitted-token analysis.
