# Autoregressive Binding Template Study Overview

## Purpose

This investigation absorbs two dense waves of Codex diagnostic records into the OKF research tree. The goal is to keep the mechanistic evidence discoverable without leaving hundreds of one-off Markdown reports on the active reading path.

The study asks how autoregressive detection outputs enter wrong object-binding, coordinate-basin, cursor, or continuation regimes, and which hidden-state, attention-route, value-source, and prefix-history interventions can move those regimes.

## Investigation Arc

The first wave established the mechanism vocabulary. It moved from broad residual patching to layer/head/source attribution, then split the observed behavior into duplicate-basin routing, false-negative coordinate guidance, and pre-onset duplication precursors. That wave had already been compressed into three synthesis notes; this OKF pass indexes the raw source notes and lifts their conclusions into the research tree without resurrecting all fragments.

The second wave continued the same scientific line after the OKF pilot. It localized object-step coordinate basins, tested adapter and surface decompositions, introduced coordinate target direction patches, examined formation-time state transitions, and later connected prefix-denoising case studies to x1/y1 route and coordinate-basin behavior.
