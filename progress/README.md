# Retained early evidence

This is a closed collection of early CoordExp evidence. The remaining records
have specific uses below: original measured comparisons, rejected mechanisms,
unexecuted alternatives, or technical counterexamples whose detailed conditions
are needed to interpret the result. Dates, old paths and recorded decisions are
historical; they do not authorize continuation or establish current behavior.

Start current work at [main documentation](../docs/README.md) or the
[canonical research questions](/data/CoordExp/.worktrees/research-probes/research/index.md).
The [documentation policy](../docs/documentation.md) owns retention and recovery.
Do not add new progress records.

| Evidence needed | Retained collection |
|---|---|
| Original coordinate, repetition, FN and prefix-state contrasts | [Diagnostics](diagnostics/README.md) |
| Checkpoint/decode comparisons and their denominators | [Benchmarks](benchmarks/README.md) |
| Proposed mechanisms and competing designs, distinct from executed results | [Directions](directions/README.md) |
| Loss-accounting, integration and acceptance counterexamples | [Audits](audits/README.md) |
| Early Stage-1 objective assumptions and ablation evidence | [Pretraining](pretrain/README.md) |

Category readers identify each retained record's independent use. Supporting
[artifact bundles](diagnostics/artifacts/README.md) retain the selected tables,
human review and raw evidence needed to audit those records. Generated reports,
configs, raw data and frozen evidence are not a second current documentation tree.
