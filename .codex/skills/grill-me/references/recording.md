# Recording A Resolved Decision

Write only resolved outcomes, in the narrowest existing authoritative location.
Do not create a new document when an active spec, research note, configuration,
test, or artifact already owns the decision.

```md
## Decision
{Choice, rationale, and consequence.}

## Evidence
- Handles: {files, artifacts, tests, metrics, or commits}
- Limits: {what remains unverified}

## Next Step
{one concrete action, owner, and stop condition}
```

Recording never implies implementation permission.
