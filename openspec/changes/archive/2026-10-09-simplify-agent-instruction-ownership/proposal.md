## Why

CoordExp's instruction system repeats generic engineering and workflow rules while WebCodex's actual project loading is uneven: main receives the constitution, infrastructure receives no project instructions, and research receives only its local entry. Reduce recurring context and competing authority without weakening scientific, data-integrity, runtime, or user-authorization boundaries.

## What Changes

- Refine the user-wide constitution; keep user preferences and high-cost invariants, not tool tutorials or CoordExp layouts.
- Give each canonical checkout a thin routing entry with a read-if-missing pointer to the single constitution. Do not claim a pointer is automatic loader expansion.
- Remove four generic wrappers (codebase-design, reclaim-code-entropy, scope-economy-review, handoff), consolidate overlapping review/delegation procedures, and shorten on-demand research execution guidance without changing its scientific contract.
- Keep specialized execution, provenance, visualization, runtime, accounting and worker-transport resources. Preserve all upstream OpenSpec packages and lifecycle behavior.
- Record the complete live inventory, ownership/migration map, discovery limitations and verification. Do not repair a loader's deliberate symlink rejection by copying or weakening package trust.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
None. This is instruction/documentation maintenance, not a new product capability.

### Modified Capabilities
None. Existing runtime, data, model, research, publication and OpenSpec requirements remain unchanged; `skip_specs: true` records that boundary. The current capability inventory was inspected before this decision.
