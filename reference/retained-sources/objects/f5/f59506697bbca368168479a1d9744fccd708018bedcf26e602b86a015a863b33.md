# History, repetition and stopping: what state does a row leave behind?

**Question:** do output changes follow image evidence, supplied token history, checkpoint parameters, or their interaction? A short non-repeating prefix is not proof of escape from later recurrence.

## Fixed history exposure, withdrawal and sampled escape

The [accepted history/pulse/sampling package](../experiments/2026-09-17-repetition-history-mechanism/results.md) separates conditional exposure effects from useful continuation. Their common escape from native cannot be assigned to the earlier A/B replacements because the native final route also differs. One supported alternate A extent preserves all37 free tokens, which does not establish abstract owner encoding. A-for-B substitution adds A and removes B, and a flat or negative response on this construction is not a general rejection of self-reinforcement.

On309264, full-time norm succeeds while finite pulses and late sustained norm fail despite changed coordinate decisions. This shows schedule dependence under the frozen boundaries, not an irreversible state or a necessity for continuous correction. Six exact pulse-token replays reproduce every batch member, confirming that output-side intervention acts through the resulting token history. Three known and four credible unlabeled free identities coexist with a repeated white bird, imperfect extents and unresolved regions. The primary physical retention denominator is empty after symmetric supplied-A exclusion; no autonomous-retention or population-recovery rate is established. ## Reopening condition

The [2026-09-17 human-review discussion and fresh128 CPU census](../experiments/2026-09-17-readout-norm-fresh128/human-review-duplication-discussion.md) prioritize distinct-owner retention under repetition suppression. They separate literal loops, near-identical geometry, physical same-owner re-emission, and legitimate overlapping instances. Exact valid repetitions586→37 are concentrated in few images; bird supplies a counterexample to a person/produce/books-only account. The visualization's IoU>=.30 pair hints are not duplicate-owner counts. The complete-row, history/pulse, selected-row, historical-row and wrapper-factorial follow-ups above are completed; a further study must distinguish explanations of the surviving conditional routes. The older native spatial-history signal and unsuccessful recovery claims from dedup remain required predecessors.

A causal state proposal must make a prediction that image, history, parameter and generic perturbation alternatives do not share. Preserve exact token history and remaining budget; distinguish same text from intervened KV. Use actual full free consequences and appropriate identity/magnitude/native-replay controls when they discriminate the named hypothesis, not as a compulsory large matrix.

Do not require a new special token, slot or ledger merely because a conditional intervention works. For a learned deployment claim, the useful effect must survive ordinary-parameter, original-image, empty-prefix generation without an inference-time oracle.
