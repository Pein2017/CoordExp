# Research: trustworthy natural set completion

## Objective and fast catch-up

Reduce physical false negatives on unlabeled or incompletely labeled images; benchmark mAP is secondary. The intended destination is original-image, empty-prefix natural greedy that covers more real owners while retaining credible old owners and avoiding false instances, repetition, invalid geometry and abnormal stopping. A complete teacher bank, sampling union, useful supplied route and naturally realized output are different things. An explicit internal owner ledger is neither assumed nor established.

## Evaluation and learning boundaries

- Keep physical existence/identity, visibility, category, geometry and part/group granularity separate. A valid individual box can contain neighbors. Real frame clipping can produce0/999; tiny size or low visibility alone does not prove absence.
- Keep original annotations and each frozen evaluation identity. Future exclusions or revised obligations require an explicit version applied symmetrically; do not delete raw GT or rewrite accepted scores. Crowds/ignore semantics need separate handling from ordinary unmatched labels.
- Newly verified training owners require explicit admission and the corresponding annotation JSONL `unlabeled` update. Discussion observations and confirmation review do not automatically become labels. Unknown physical identity and unknown category are separate.
- CE masks remove direct target loss, not conditioning history or all downstream influence. Removing an unknown row changes the suffix's evidence condition. An aggregate trajectory reward does not by itself give correct credit to every row.
- Fewer duplicate boxes, fewer red overlays, a longer output, better mean loss or a larger owner union do not establish better free enumeration. Post-hoc NMS cannot recover missed owners or spent generation budget.
- [TIDE-aligned review vocabulary](../docs/eval/UNMATCHED_REVIEW.md) remains the project convention. The [closed Co-DETR diagnostic and user adjudication](experiments/2026-09-16-codetr-only-review-proxy/results.md) ([state](experiments/2026-09-16-codetr-only-review-proxy/state.json)) support screening, not automatic GT. Co-DETR is the preferred detector proxy if needed, with no default VLM judge; proxy calibration is an optional support axis, not the next experiment's prerequisite.
