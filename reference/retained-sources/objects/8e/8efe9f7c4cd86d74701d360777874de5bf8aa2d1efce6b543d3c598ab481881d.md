## C. Greedy recurrence and conditional event probability

All 45 original native states completed 256 full-vocabulary ancestral draws each: 11,520 short draws. The fixed legal same-description near-repeat event occurred 441 times. The denominator retains 939 other invalid extents, 4 invalid near-repeat outcomes and 17 grammar escapes; they are not retried or conditioned away. All 29 saved greedy next rows that satisfy the legal event are identified separately from the remaining 16.

For selected failure states, empirical q ranges 0.0117–0.1094 tied (median 0.0273) and 0–0.2266 untied (median 0.0625), with per-state Wilson intervals. Thus sustained native greedy recurrence can coexist with relatively low mass on this particular complete numerical event. This does not imply that sampling repairs enumeration: the event is conditioned on an explicitly supplied next-row description/wrapper, and 1−q includes other owners, other repeats, invalidity and format escape. It is not new-owner probability.

Seven prospective tied proxies have a different next-row description and an empty same-description historical union. Their q=0 is structural, not evidence of suppression. The raw `source_policy=null` fields for new-cohort records are metadata omissions resolved by the immutable shared-panel/source crosswalk. Image-level summaries preserve the 23 distinct image identities; pooled fractions are descriptive and have no claimed cross-image binomial interval.

## D. Input geometry and fixed-suffix sensitivity

Actual initialization is `natural_adjacent`, 1,000 bins, 8 bands, scale 0.02, seed 0. Its highest-band adjacent phase is about 0.805 rad; periodic features coincide at 0/999, while the linear feature and projected/trained rows differ. Exact initializer reconstruction passes. Smoothness does not imply slow variation or distance monotonicity. Trained input rows and output rows were audited separately: mean adjacent input L2 is 0.228 tied and 0.247 untied; untied input and output rows differ.

The frozen audit has 16 states, 32 ±1 contrasts and 64 immediate/later observations. All selected edits are y2, preserving measured validity/order. Hook/no-hook outputs are bitwise identical; actual input deltas equal the corresponding trained-row differences. One of 32 immediate winners changes and none at the later fixed boundary. Full-vocabulary centered-logit change and coordinate-family conditional-distribution change are separately reconstructed. The pooled median centered full-vocabulary maximum-change/native-margin ratio is 0.251 immediate and 0.476 later, with substantial stratum variation.

This selected fixed-suffix evidence supports mostly stable winners despite measurable propagated changes, with one small-margin switch. It cannot establish free-trajectory stability, semantic equivalence of a one-bin edit, all-role behavior, or a causal layer origin. Growing residual differences are descriptive; no layer/KV patch was performed.

## Integrated limit and acceptance

The observations jointly constrain a simple endpoint-only account: recurrence appears in multiple categories and interior locations; both image and coordinate history affect conditional spatial scores; greedy recurrence need not carry high probability for a complete numerical repeat event; actual input geometry is structured and can propagate changes without routinely changing the next winner. Competing accounts involving conditional history, visual context, route/format dynamics and small-margin selection remain. No result establishes an abstract owner ledger, unique internal circuit, training-origin defect, physical recovery or deployable repair.
