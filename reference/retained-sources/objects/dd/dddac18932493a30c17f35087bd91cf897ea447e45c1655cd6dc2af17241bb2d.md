## Independent recommendation A

/root/research_direction_a retained a trainable row-end continuous-feedback pilot as the primary new direction, with an optional smaller native-state diagnostic. Its principal corrections were:
- The contrast tests a feedback path at a fixed supervision dose, not isolated persistence or sample efficiency.
- Native transplantation is useful only if it adds reciprocal owner selectivity, same-owner expression robustness and delay beyond the completed history diagnostic.
- The training bank must support credible post-coverage successors; the old entry bank alone cannot be assumed sufficient.
- Feedback must be constructed causally through earlier rows. A target-fork-only injection tests a different local intervention.
- The 24 GPU-hour suggestion is a cap without a validated workload estimate.

## Independent recommendation B

/root/research_direction_b also preferred the paired architecture pilot, with a bounded content-use check on the trained feedback route instead of front-loading another native KV panel. Its additional corrections were:
- An ordinary slot has a constant input embedding but contextual continuous states and K/V. Both arms already possess history pathways.
- A parameter-free normalized feedback mapping with identical trainable parameter sets is a plausible minimal candidate, not yet an approved implementation.
- Every completed row must receive the same internal-slot protocol in training and natural generation, including model mistakes.
- Prefix replay, masks, positions, target prediction alignment and gradient propagation must preserve the proposed causal graph.
- Native teacher protection needs an explicit mapping to visible-token decisions in the slot-augmented protocol; it is distillation across protocols, not same-input parity.
- Equal example exposure and equal compute are different comparisons. Measure actual work.
- A separate endpoint must preserve the active task's data and confirmation roles.

## Lead disposition

Accept both reports as useful scientific advice, not as experimental results or evidence of model-family superiority.

Revise the primary question to: from the same frozen N16 anchor and admitted successor supervision, does an additional row-end-to-input continuous feedback route improve complete natural enumeration over an ordinary contextual slot at the same training exposure?

Favor two independently trained arms and one fixed comparison. Treat correct-versus-replaced feedback as optional supporting evidence about route use; it must not veto the primary trial. Defer a separate native-KV sweep.

Do not infer an owner ledger, missing native capacity, general persistence, or a sample-efficiency curve from this pilot. A mechanically valid null closes this implementation and dose; a state/gradient failure leaves the scientific question unanswered.

The lead checked the original cost record: the previous single N16 fit used approximately 22.745 allocated GPU-hours. This does not predict the new implementation, but 24 GPU-hours cannot yet be presented as sufficient for two fits and evaluation.
