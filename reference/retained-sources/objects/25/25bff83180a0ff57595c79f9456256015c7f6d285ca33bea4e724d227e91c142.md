# GT vs Prediction

Left panel draws GT; right panel draws predictions.
Green means matched, yellow means missing GT, red means unmatched prediction.
Solid outlines are the polygons used for mask-IoU matching; cyan dashed outlines are non-rectangular polygon envelopes.
GT boxes are converted from norm1000 bins to pixel rectangles; prediction polygons are already pixels.
