## Mathematical audit claims

These are exact statements about the diagnostic surface, not claims about the
model's internal mechanism.

### A VLM state key includes the image

For this audit an exact state key is:

\[
s=(H_{image},H_{prompt},H_{prefix},b),
\]

where the three hashes identify image content, executed prompt tokens, and
prefix tokens, and `b` identifies the row boundary.  Grouping only by prefix
token hash falsely merges different visual states, especially empty prefixes.

### Different owners at one state are incompatible immediate actions

Let two complete owner rows at the same exact state first differ at token
position `u`.  A deterministic greedy policy has one argmax at that position,
so it cannot realize both rows as the same immediate next action.  Same-owner
aliases are alternatives and are not counted as this cross-owner conflict.
This establishes an objective incompatibility only when both candidates carry
material weight; grouped soft-min may instead select one dominant row.

### Full-row margin and state reachability are separate requirements

For row `a=(a_1,...,a_L)` at exact state `s`, let `z` be the logits after the
same generation processors used by the registered greedy policy and define

\[
m_u(a,s;\theta)
=z_\theta(a_u\mid s,a_{<u})
-\max_{v\ne a_u}z_\theta(v\mid s,a_{<u}).
\]

If every required row token has `m_u>0`, induction over `u` proves that greedy
decoding from `s` emits the complete row.  This fixed-state certificate is not
enough for natural realization: the current policy must first reach `s`.
Therefore the audit reports both

\[
\text{natural reachability of }s
\quad\text{and}\quad
\min_u m_u(a,s;\theta)>0.
\]

Teacher-forced margin improvement without state reachability is a prefix
cascade diagnosis, not a natural-owner success.

### Mean preservation can hide a failed greedy boundary

For a monotone margin loss `ell`, reducing

\[
J^{-1}\sum_j \ell(m_j)
\]

does not imply `m_j>0` for every incumbent.  For logistic loss, margins changing
from `(0.1,0.1)` to `(10,-0.01)` lower the mean loss while the second decision
crosses the greedy boundary.  This mathematical possibility is not yet evidence
that it occurred in D0; the per-owner audit below must show it on the frozen
specimen.

## Audit A1: train-side natural realization

This is the primary missing observation.

Fresh-cold Source, C, and D0 natural greedy decodes must cover the same 248
eligible optimization images under the predecessor prompt, repetition penalty
`1.0`, temperature `0`, ordinary natural EOS, and the same generation cap and
parser.  The old Source capture may be reused only if prompt, image, generation,
token, parser, and cap identities match exactly; otherwise Source is decoded
again.

Report at IoU50/60/80:

- annotated-owner count, owner-micro recall, and image-macro recall;
- uptake of owners named by positive base events;
- retention of Source-covered owners, with preservation-event coverage as a
  separate subset;
- gained and lost owner identities relative to Source and C;
- generated rows, duplicates, valid unmatched rows, ordering violations,
  geometry-invalid or malformed drops, natural EOS, and cap/nontermination.

Interpretation:

- train-side D0 gain with screen regression supports training-state
  specialization or transfer failure, but does not uniquely prove lookup;
- fixed-prefix gain without natural train gain supports reachability or action
  realization failure;
- no fixed-prefix or natural gain closes the claim that D0 had usable positive
  signal under its executed objective;
- C's superiority remains confounded with its much larger coherent supervision
  dose.

## Audit A2: state-correct multiplicity and winner stability

Operate on unique base events; dose repeats are reported separately and never
create new support.  Group by the full VLM state key, then distinguish:

- one owner with multiple complete aliases;
- multiple physical owners sharing a state;
- first divergent token type: description/category, coordinate, closure, or
  EOS/delimiter;
- candidate loss gap, grouped-softmin weight, hard winner, and winner switch
  from Source to D0.

Multiplicity is descriptive.  Report top-two normalized grouped weights and
winner switches rather than manufacturing a binary conflict threshold after
seeing the cardinalities; raw `K>1` alone is insufficient.  Even when
unsupported as a causal diagnosis, one owner per current state remains a
conservative future materializer rule.

## Audit A3: C-anchor prefix reachability

Using the fresh C optimization-set trajectories, classify every Source-bank
boundary within the same image and prompt as:

1. exact token-prefix reachable;
2. token-different but the same locked physical-owner set and row boundary;
3. owner-set or row-boundary different;
4. absent from C's natural trajectory.

Exact token mismatch and physical equivalence are separate outputs.  No `>50%`
admission threshold is retained: any C-anchored treatment must build its initial
bank from C regardless.  This audit determines whether predecessor events can
be reused at all and informs later refresh cadence; it does not by itself select
active-set projection.

## Audit A5: positive realization and preservation-tail lesions

Stored generated-token traces contain no runner-up logits, so fresh no-update
teacher-forced scoring is required.

### Optimization-event surface

At every unique positive and preservation base event, score Source and D0 on
the exact Source state and complete candidate row.  Report:

- per-candidate sequence loss and grouped-softmin weight;
- weakest full-row token margin and token type;
- hard candidate winner and Source-to-D0 winner switch;
- whether the complete row is greedily certified at that fixed state;
- per-event preservation CE and weakest-margin change;
- linkage to the A1 natural gained, retained, or lost owner outcome.

A concrete **surrogate** counterexample requires:

1. the Source incumbent row is certified at its Source state;
2. its D0 weakest critical margin crosses to `<=0` while aggregate preservation
   loss decreases;
3. artifact and prefix identities are exact.

Call it a **behavior-linked lesion** only when the corresponding owner is also
lost in D0's natural optimization-set decode and D0 reaches the tested Source
prefix up to that critical decision.  If D0 diverges earlier, classify the case
as a prefix cascade; the fixed-state crossing is not claimed to cause the
natural loss.

Matched retained-owner controls are reported.  A single valid counterexample
proves that mean preservation supplied no per-owner guarantee; prevalence and
active-set capacity still determine whether projection is a useful next step.

### Old-screen behavioral surface

The correct preservation lesion cohort is the 28 Source-covered owners lost by
D0, compared with the 586 Source owners retained by D0.  Stratify both by C
status.  The 43 C-covered/D0-lost owners are not a preservation cohort: 23 are
Source incumbents and 20 are C-only gains that D0's Source-preservation
objective never named.

Screen scoring may locate C/D0 natural divergence and fixed-state critical
margins, but it is a collateral-behavior diagnostic only.  There is no
per-screen-owner preservation CE, and no causal lesion claim is permitted.

## Strongest alternatives

1. **Coherent scaffold or supervision density.**  C saw about 11.9 times more
   supervised row/token information.  The practical later control is continued
   C training; a token-matched causal arm is deferred unless attribution, rather
   than algorithm selection, becomes decision-owning.
2. **Positive action never became greedily executable.**  Lower grouped loss
   can coexist with one negative critical token margin.  A2/A5 separate this
   from prefix reachability.
3. **Training-state specialization.**  D0 may improve its own optimization
   images yet damage the old screen.  A1 is the cheapest discriminator.
4. **One capped trajectory dominates debt.**  A4 already shows this for raw
   drops; cap and uncapped parser burden remain separate.
5. **Exact token staleness without semantic loss.**  A3 preserves
   physical-owner-equivalent boundaries as a separate class rather than calling
   every token mismatch off-policy failure.

## Decision matrix and stop rules

| Observation | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Any model, image, prompt, owner, prefix, adapter, parser, or artifact mismatch | `MECHANICAL_INVALID_C_ANCHORED_AUDIT`; stop and preserve the failed receipt. |
| D0 has neither fixed-state positive improvement nor train-side natural target uptake, but correctly attributed candidate signal remains available | `GO_C_ANCHORED_SIMPLE_FEASIBILITY` only for a tiny complete-action objective screen; no active set or full CP. |
| Fixed-state positive margins improve but train natural owners do not | Route a current-prefix/action-realization feasibility screen; do not build projection. |
| Train target uptake exists, but no exact incumbent tail-margin counterexample is found | Active-set rationale stops; at most test a simpler current-prefix single-successor trust-region arm. |
| Train target uptake and at least one exact natural owner loss with an aggregate-hidden critical-margin crossing are verified | `GO_C_ANCHORED_ACTIVE_SET_FEASIBILITY`, subject to bounded capacity below. |
| Supported threatened incumbents cannot fit an image-balanced active set of at most 16 | Hold rather than silently enlarge the active set. |
| No correctly attributed candidate signal remains, or existing evidence is fully explained by C's coherent supervision advantage | `SCIENTIFIC_STOP_C_ANCHORED_SUCCESSOR`. |

A following active-set feasibility unit must first compare the unconstrained
C-anchor gain direction with critical incumbent gradients.  If the direction
does not threaten incumbents, projection is unnecessary.  If it does, only a
small no-update global dual QP predicting a nonzero feasible direction may
authorize one projected-update vertical slice.  A zero or ambiguous direction
is a local-feasibility stop, not permission to increase learning rate, active
set size, rank, parameter surface, or dose.
