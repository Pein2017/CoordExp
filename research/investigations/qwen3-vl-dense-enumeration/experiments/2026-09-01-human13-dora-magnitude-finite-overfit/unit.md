# Human13 DoRA Magnitude Finite Overfit

## Why this successor exists

The completed Human13 shared output result already answered the first capacity
question: one output-only residual shared across the fixed panel can compile
`65/65`, `123/123`, and `392/392` under fresh-cold natural greedy. That is
finite-panel shared-output compilability only.

The later scalable shared-DoRA route stopped earlier for a different reason:
its registered cross-image whole-bundle null was structurally infeasible before
any gradient or optimizer step. That `SCIENTIFIC_STOP` remains closed and
authoritative for that control route. It is not reopened, softened, or reused
here.

This unit owns the user-corrected direct successor:

> From the exact unmerged step-2444 Source, can changing only the existing 196
> shared language-DoRA magnitude vectors compile the fixed Human13 policy
> through one real unmerged adapter, first on N2, then N4, then N13?

The earlier magnitude-QP bridge plan remains provenance only. Exact
permutation, matched-swap, and QP/Farkas work are deferred diagnostics, not
admission gates for this unit.

## Active contract

> From the exact unmerged step-2444 Source and the frozen Human13
> `geo_sorted_xy` panel, does a magnitude-only update shared across all 196
> language-DoRA vectors produce one unmerged adapter that (a) replays the full
> canonical target route over the full vocabulary with the registered margin and
> (b) then decodes the same panel under fresh natural greedy with complete owner
> coverage, zero hard debt, and natural EOS on the nested `N2 -> N4 -> N13`
> ladder?

This is a fixed-panel finite programmability question only. A successful result
would show internal-network magnitude-surface overfit on this panel. It would
not show held-out transfer, population generalization, semantic sharing,
ordinary SGD learnability beyond the frozen pilot, or superiority over full
DoRA.

A change to the Source, panel, stage membership, trainable surface, replay
margin, natural evaluator, or stop rule is a phase reset.

## Contrast, evidence path, and strongest alternative

The within-stage contrast is always:

```text
unchanged Source  versus  Source + one shared magnitude-only adapter update
```

The decision-bearing evidence path is:

1. exact Source and surface admission;
2. magnitude-only finite training on the registered stage subset;
3. full-vocabulary canonical replay on the real unmerged wrapper;
4. adapter-only save plus fresh base-and-unmerged-adapter readback;
5. fresh natural-greedy owner/debt/EOS evaluation.

The strongest alternative is replay-only memorization that improves the
teacher-forced canonical route but fails to compile a stable fresh natural
policy. That is why canonical replay alone cannot pass a stage.

## Objective and finite attempt budget

The smallest discoverable existing training seam is full-vocabulary
cross-entropy on the canonical replay route. This unit therefore freezes the
objective family to:

```text
full-vocab canonical replay cross-entropy with the standard adapter-only optimizer surface
```

This is an implementation-owned pilot setting, not a separate scientific claim.
The scientific gate is finite full-vocabulary replay plus fresh natural greedy.
Smooth max-margin, QP, or matched-swap objectives are outside this unit unless
the user opens a fresh phase.

The exact optimizer hyperparameters, step count, and regularization values are
not scientifically privileged here and are therefore launch-packet-owned. The
launch packet must freeze one deterministic schedule before N2 and reuse it
unchanged through N13 unless a stage stops earlier. This unit permits:

- one scientific training attempt per stage under that frozen schedule;
- at most one same-schedule rerun after a demonstrated mechanical repair; and
- no learning-rate sweep, no objective swap, no stage-specific retuning, and no
  adaptive checkpoint selection inside the unit.

### Stage -1: identity and zero-drift admission

Before any backward pass:

1. re-hash the Source, panel, prompt/config, tokenizer, and selected-token
   delta;
2. prove that exactly the 196 magnitude vectors are trainable and every other
   tensor is frozen;
3. bind the canonical target-token ledger for the current stage;
4. reproduce unchanged Source natural greedy at RP1.0, with RP1.10 recorded as
   a nonblocking monitor; and
5. prove save/read/load restoration leaves the Source adapter bytes unchanged
   when no update is applied.

Any failure is `MECHANICAL_INVALID`.

### Stage N2, N4, N13

Each stage starts from the exact Source; a previous stage's learned adapter is
never the next stage's initialization.

For the current stage, a pass requires all of the following:

1. **Magnitude-only training validity:** only the 196 magnitude vectors moved.
2. **Finite canonical replay over full vocab:** on the real unmerged wrapper,
   every canonical target token, including terminal EOS, has
   target-versus-full-vocabulary margin at least `0.01 - 2e-5`.
3. **Adapter-only persistence:** save exactly the adapter payload, reload it
   onto a fresh base-plus-selected-token-delta wrapper, and reproduce the same
   finite canonical margins.
4. **Fresh natural greedy:** at RP1.0, decode from the original prompt without
   teacher forcing and reach strict same-category, global one-to-one owner
   coverage of `65/65`, `123/123`, or `392/392` at IoU50, with IoU60/80
   reported, zero duplicate/unmatched/malformed/invalid-box/token-cap hard
   debt, and natural EOS on every image.

Exact canonical route replay under natural greedy is a diagnostic, not an
additional hard gate beyond owner/debt/EOS completion.

N4 may launch only after N2 passes. N13 may launch only after N4 passes. A
failed smaller stage stops the ladder.

## Failure classification

- `MECHANICAL_INVALID`: Source, panel, wrapper, trainable-surface, save/load,
  hash, parser, or evaluator identity drift.
- `M_ONLY_OPTIMIZATION_FAIL`: the frozen schedule ends without a candidate that
  satisfies the finite full-vocabulary replay gate for the current stage. This
  rejects the registered optimization attempt, not magnitude-only
  representability and not full DoRA.
- `M_ONLY_REPLAY_PASS_NATURAL_FAIL`: full-vocabulary replay and fresh readback
  pass, but fresh natural greedy misses the owner/debt/EOS gate. This is
  evidence of a representation-to-policy gap on this finite surface at this
  stage; it still does not reject full DoRA.
- `N2_PASS`, `N4_PASS`, `N13_PASS`: the current stage passes all finite replay,
  persistence, and natural gates.

No negative outcome in this unit may be promoted into “full DoRA cannot do
this.” Full `A/B/m` DoRA, different objectives, and different same-panel
controls remain separate questions.
