# Sorted All-Person Owner-Relative Route Landscape

## Decision and claim boundary

This prospective successor to the
[sorted false-negative mechanism decomposition](../2026-08-02-sorted-false-negative-mechanism-decomposition/unit.md)
measures a descriptive owner-relative likelihood landscape for all confirmed
`person` owners in image `7511`. It asks whether one owner's exact-anchor or
local-neighborhood score changes across root, exact sampled self-due, and an
exact counterfactual skip-post prefix when every owner receives the same
score-independent candidate budget. It does not identify a causal route,
coverage, or scan mechanism and does not choose or promote an architecture.

The decision unit is a confirmed physical owner. The primary targets are
`gt:7511:17` and `gt:7511:22`; `gt:7511:2` and `gt:7511:32` are additional
self-due calibration owners. All forty-one confirmed person owners remain in
the primary scored universe in every admitted context.

The strongest permitted claim is:

> Under one frozen canonical-person complete-box interface and one equal owner
> bank, a target was or was not a winner at its sampled self-due context, and
> its owner-relative rank or margin changed selectively across exact prefixes;
> realized-box sidecars separately show whether the finite equal bank covers a
> previously observed model realization.

The due-to-skip contrast changes scan frontier, owner content, route depth, and
conditioning jointly. Any observed shift is therefore route-conditioned and
descriptive. Scan progression, generic extra-row conditioning, covered-owner
suppression, and other prefix interactions remain unresolved. This is a
within-image, within-checkpoint case study, not a prevalence estimate, an
object-existence probability, a proof of visual representation, a negative
localization or absence claim, or evidence that a training or architecture
treatment will improve natural final-set coverage. Finite candidate-bank or
sampling failure stays unresolved.

## Falsifiable question and alternatives

When all forty-one confirmed person owners receive the same deterministic
local candidate budget, which owners win at each sampled self-due context, and
does target `gt:7511:17` or `gt:7511:22` show a target-selective descriptive
margin decline in an exact skip-post or same-length owner-swap comparison?

Three non-causal patterns are reported separately:

1. **scan-proximity-compatible:** owner score ranks covary with distance from
   the last complete emitted `person` row;
2. **GT-extent-sensitive:** the exact GT anchor ranks poorly while an equal-bank
   extent perturbation wins for that owner; and
3. **prefix-content-sensitive:** the target's margin changes more than every
   eligible uncovered non-target owner under the frozen selectivity rule.

These patterns may coexist. None is relabelled as route-state collision. The
smallest decision-changing observation is the target's root/due/post
neighborhood-max rank and margin, exact-anchor rank, ambiguity bounds,
realized-sidecar gap, and the same-length owner-swap diagnostic.

## Shared owner-only candidate universe

Let `O` be the forty-one confirmed person owners. Every owner contributes one
exact GT anchor and `Q = 8` local perturbations, for exactly nine token-distinct
complete-box candidates per owner and `369` logical score rows per context.
The same frozen `369` candidate IDs and coordinate tokens are scored in every
context. There are no background boxes, synthetic decoys, natural prediction
extras, or score-selected candidates.

Candidate generation occurs before any model score is read and uses the
production pixel-to-coordinate-token conversion. For owner width `w` and height
`h` in decoded pixels, set `dx = max(1, round(w / 4))` and
`dy = max(1, round(h / 4))`. After clipping to the image canvas and converting
to coordinate tokens, select candidates deterministically as follows:

1. retain the exact GT anchor;
2. select four distinct translation candidates from the fixed order left,
   right, up, down, up-left, up-right, down-left, down-right, using offsets
   `dx` and `dy` while preserving width and height when the canvas permits;
3. select four further distinct extent candidates from the fixed order width
   expand, width shrink, height expand, height shrink, isotropic expand,
   isotropic shrink, width-expand/height-shrink, and
   width-shrink/height-expand, using symmetric per-side changes `dx` and `dy`;
4. reject invalid or token-duplicate boxes and continue down the stated order
   within the same family until the quota is filled.

Selection is against candidate identity only, never score. If an owner cannot
produce four translation and four extent candidates that are valid, distinct
after tokenization, and distinct from its exact anchor, the representative
smoke stops for a protocol amendment; the implementation must not silently
change `Q`, borrow another owner's candidates, or introduce background.

Each logical candidate keeps its generating owner and transform provenance,
but physical assignment is recomputed geometrically against all forty-one
owners. Primary point estimates use only candidates with exactly one strict
owner assignment. A candidate generated from one owner but strictly assigned
only to another belongs to the latter owner for analysis and is not silently
kept by its generator. Ambiguous multi-owner candidates remain in receipts and
enter only the frozen lower/upper assignment bounds defined below.

## Frozen measurements

For context `c` and complete-box candidate `b`, let `s_c(b)` be its raw
complete-box log likelihood.

### Numerical tolerance and ranks

Before reading any target contrast, score the exact GT anchor of the
lowest-index owner `gt:7511:2` eight times with uncached scalar FP32 full
reforward at `self-due-gt17`. If the repeated scores are `r_1,...,r_8`, define

`delta = max_i |r_i - median(r)|` and `epsilon = max(1e-6, 2 * delta)`.

The candidate, repeat count, context, formula, and lower floor are frozen and
target-blind. Batched execution is admitted only after scalar parity within
`epsilon`.

For owner `o`:

- **exact-anchor score** is `s_c` of `o`'s exact GT anchor when that anchor is
  unambiguously assigned to `o`;
- **exact-anchor rank** is its competition rank among the unambiguous exact GT
  anchors of all confirmed owners;
- **neighborhood maximum** is the maximum `s_c` over all unambiguous shared-bank
  candidates assigned to `o`, including its exact anchor when admissible;
- **neighborhood-max rank** is its competition rank among all owners having an
  admissible neighborhood maximum; and
- **target margin** is the target neighborhood maximum minus the largest
  neighborhood maximum of any other confirmed owner.

For either exact or neighborhood maxima `M_c(o)`, define

`rank_c(o) = 1 + count_j[M_c(j) > M_c(o) + epsilon]`.

Report tied-owner count `count_j[|M_c(j)-M_c(o)| <= epsilon]`, the fixed eligible
denominator, and every best-other owner ID. Margin is positive only above
`epsilon`, negative only below `-epsilon`, and tied otherwise. A difference of
two context margins is nonzero only when its magnitude exceeds `2 * epsilon`.

Exact-anchor and neighborhood-max ranks are always reported in separate
columns and never pooled or substituted for each other. Report the best-other
owner ID with every margin. Report rank numerators and eligible denominators;
owners without an admissible candidate remain neutral, not worst-ranked.

### Ambiguity bounds

For each owner define `L_c(o)` as its maximum over uniquely assigned primary
candidates and `U_c(o)` as the maximum after also allowing every ambiguous
candidate that strict-matches `o`. The optimistic and pessimistic bounds are:

- best rank: `1 + count_j[L_c(j) > U_c(o) + epsilon]`;
- worst rank: `1 + count_j[U_c(j) > L_c(o) + epsilon]`;
- lower margin: `L_c(o) - max_{j != o} U_c(j)`; and
- upper margin: `U_c(o) - max_{j != o} L_c(j)`.

A winner, loser, sign, or selective-change statement is admitted only when its
status is invariant across these bounds. Before GPU work, every primary target
and self-due calibration owner must retain at least five of its nine primary
candidates as uniquely assigned; otherwise the unit stops for a candidate-bank
amendment.

### Selectivity and scan diagnostics

For due/post target `t`, define the one-versus-rest owner margin
`m_c(o) = M_c(o) - max_{j != o} M_c(j)` and
`D_o = m_post(o) - m_due(o)`. The control set contains owners absent from both
prefixes, excluding `t` and any owner whose ambiguity bounds do not admit the
same status. A target decline is **target-selective descriptive change** only
when

`D_t < min_{o in controls} D_o - 2 * epsilon`.

Otherwise it is a generic context shift or unresolved. This rule does not turn
the splice into a causal intervention.

For each non-root context, take the last complete donor `person` row, whether
matched or unmatched, and insert its `(y1, x1)` into the GT owner order sorted
by `(y1, x1, owner_id)`. Report every owner's signed and absolute ordinal
distance from that frontier and Spearman correlation between owner score rank
and absolute scan distance, with average ranks for ties. The scan statistic is
diagnostic only; root has no frontier statistic.

For each context also report the full forty-one-owner rank table, `(y1, x1)`
scan order, GT width and height, the winning candidate transform, and the
realized-sidecar gap. This permits direct review without adding a post-hoc
candidate bank.

### Calibration outcomes and primary tables

Prefix identity, exact token parity, candidate integrity, and ambiguity
retention are admission controls. Sampled next-owner behavior is not proof that
the complete-sequence likelihood winner is rank `1`; therefore the four
self-due ranks are reported outcomes, not an all-or-nothing admission gate.
Each due owner receives `winner_at_due`, `tied_at_due`, or `not_winner_at_due`.
The four contexts are nested prefixes of one seed-`21010` trajectory and are
not independent statistical controls.

For compact calibration summaries, four rank-1 outcomes are `controls_strong`;
exactly one rank-2 outcome whose winning gap is at most `epsilon` is
`controls_marginal`; every other pattern is `controls_descriptive_only`. These
labels never veto scoring or imply independent replication. A target-specific
winner-to-loser observation requires that target itself have neighborhood rank
`1` and positive bounded margin at due.

The primary tables are:

- `gt:7511:17`: `root`, `self-due-gt17`, and `skip-post-gt17`;
- `gt:7511:22`: `root`, `self-due-gt22`, and `skip-post-gt22` only after that
  context is independently admitted; and
- same-length owner-swap: `self-due-gt22` versus `skip-post-gt17`, reporting
  `gt:7511:17` and `gt:7511:22` symmetrically.

Positive owner-relative due support means neighborhood rank `1` with a lower
margin bound above `epsilon`. A nonpositive, tied, or ambiguity-sensitive result
stays unresolved. No threshold or outcome is generalized outside image `7511`.

## Behavioral transfer and conditional sampling

Likelihood scoring is primary; behavior is greedy first. Run the corrected
canonical-person one-box greedy readout for all four self-due calibration cells
and for every target-positive due, post, or owner-swap cell. Force only
canonical `person` and the box opener, decode one complete box, and assign it
against the same owner ledger. Record target, other-owner, ambiguous, invalid,
or STOP outcome without changing the landscape rank.

Low-temperature sampling is not run by default. It is admitted only when:

1. the target has positive owner-relative support under the frozen rank and
   margin rule;
2. the role-matched corrected greedy readout does not unambiguously strict-match
   that target; and
3. prefix parity, identity, candidate, and ambiguity admission have passed.

The conditional sampling policy is already frozen: temperature `0.4`, top-p
`0.95`, repetition penalty `1.0`, `K = 32`, exactly one canonical-person
complete-box horizon, and no retry or adaptive extension. For role ID `r` and
sample index `k = 0,...,31`, encode the exact ASCII string
`unit_id + "|" + r + "|" + decimal(k)` as UTF-8, compute SHA-256, interpret the
first four digest bytes as one unsigned big-endian integer, and mask with
`0x7fffffff`. All 32 seeds must be unique; a collision stops without rehashing.
The exact values are materialized in a CPU receipt and independently
regenerated before the first sample. A sampled strict target hit is
sampler-conditional positive accessibility evidence. Finite sampling failure
is neutral. Neither greedy nor sampled one-box success establishes a natural
final-set gain.

## Interpretation map

- **Scan diagnostic:** report the frozen Spearman coefficient, its eligible
  owner count, and the per-owner signed/absolute ordinal distances. No
  categorical scan-proximity label or post-hoc threshold is permitted.
- **GT-extent diagnostic:** report exact-anchor rank, neighborhood rank, score
  difference, and winning transform. No categorical `poor`/`good` extent label
  or post-hoc threshold is permitted.
- **Finite-bank undercoverage:** a frozen realized sidecar exceeds the same
  owner's primary neighborhood maximum by more than `epsilon`.
- **Prefix-content-sensitive:** the target meets the frozen descriptive
  selectivity inequality, with rank/margin status invariant to ambiguity.
- **Coverage-compatible owner-swap:** `gt:7511:17` is lower when its own row is
  present than when row `4` is present instead, under equal prefix length. This
  remains non-causal because owner content and frontier differ.
- **Unresolved:** evidence is tied, nonpositive, ambiguity-sensitive, the post
  context is blocked, or more than one pattern remains compatible.

These are bounded descriptive dispositions, not mutually exclusive mechanism
truths. No disposition is named route-state collision.

## Stop rules

Stop or narrow before interpreting target root/due/post differences if:

1. checkpoint, adapter, special-token delta, image, panel, prompt, tokenizer,
   matcher, owner-ledger, or donor-rollout identity differs from the frozen
   source boundary;
2. the census does not yield exactly forty-one confirmed image-`7511` person
   owners with permanent IDs `gt:7511:2` through `gt:7511:42`;
3. any owner lacks exactly one exact anchor and eight valid, distinct,
   score-independent perturbations, or candidate IDs/tokens differ by context;
4. any mandatory self-due or `skip-post-gt17` prefix fails exact row binding,
   token reconstruction, teacher-forced parity, parser grammar, or image/prompt
   parity;
5. any target or self-due calibration owner retains fewer than five uniquely
   assigned primary candidates, the numerical-repeat receipt is missing, or a
   batch/scalar score differs beyond `epsilon`;
6. the target's winner, loser, sign, or selectivity status changes across the
   frozen ambiguity bounds;
7. target identity, sidecar provenance, or owner assignment is not unique under the canonical
   matcher;
8. target rank evidence is tied, nonpositive, or changes sign under the scalar
   numerical tolerance; the result stays unresolved rather than being promoted
   to negative evidence;
9. the proposed `skip-post-gt22` context lacks an exact approved donor-row list,
   intended retained owner, raw-span identities, or prefix-token parity; only
   that context remains blocked and no sequence is guessed;
10. corrected greedy is absent or malformed for a cell used in a behavioral
    claim, or sampling is requested without positive owner-relative target
    support, a failed role-matched corrected greedy readout, the frozen 32-seed
    receipt, one-box horizon, and no-retry policy; or
11. an interpretation would require a negative localization/absence claim,
    prevalence claim, natural final-set claim, training, annotation mutation,
    crop/rescale, architecture work, or expansion beyond image `7511`.

Prefix/parity failure, bank-integrity failure, and ambiguity-sensitive status
stop the affected interpretation. A non-rank-1 sampled calibration owner is a
reported outcome, not an automatic execution stop. Nonpositive target evidence
closes unresolved. No causal route/coverage, broad, or absence claim is
permitted from this unit.
