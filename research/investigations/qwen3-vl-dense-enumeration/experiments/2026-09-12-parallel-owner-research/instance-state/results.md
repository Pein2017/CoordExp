the positive417044coordinate-cache seam, not a new layer, head or architecture.
Reacquire the same native and A/B/background donor slices; save their full

the choice changes the estimand and remains root-owned. If control directions
also escape, abstract owner-specific interpretation weakens. If only A works,
direction specificity strengthens but is still not a durable owner-ledger proof.

without an outcome-selected layer/dose/seed search.
