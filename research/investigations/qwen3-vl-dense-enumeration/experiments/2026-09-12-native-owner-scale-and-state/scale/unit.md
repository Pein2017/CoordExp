# Scale lane

From unchanged Stable50, does one fixedP fit over 16--32 visually trusted
literal `h+c+w` packages improve natural owner recovery beyond the earlier
two-image successes while retaining natural owners?

## Frozen source and sampling

The outcome-blind source universe is the already exposed Stable50 `eval_records`
in `2026-09-11-positive-progress-matched-control/endpoint-preparation/packet.json`.
All 384 identities, not only the selected raw128, are excluded from fresh
evaluation. The corrected pre-outcome v2 raw128 consists of all 25 available
repeat/drift images, 71 salt-ranked missing/early-stop images, and 32
salt-ranked true no-obvious-failure controls with FN50=0. This intentionally
includes loops, omissions and ordinary outputs
without making donut417044 the gate or population.

Within each selected image, v2 nominates at most two literal rows
whose owner is absent at IoU50 and whose parsed box has any-class IoU at most
0.30 with every native-history box. This is a conservative nomination HOLD,
not a physical-identity theorem. Rows are GT-backed and ranked by visible pixel
area then owner ID, except that one already root-reviewed donut unlabeled model
proposal is exercised with separate provenance. Ambiguity and excess rows
remain neutral. No nomination is replaced after an acquisition failure.

The machine-authoritative selection and acquisition packets live under
`/data/CoordExp/outputs/research/qwen3-vl-dense-enumeration/2026-09-12-native-owner-scale-and-state/scale/preparation/`.

## Acquisition, admission and stop

For a strict-repeat case, `h` is the literal native prefix immediately before
the first later-row IoU>0.95 repeat, or before an earlier malformed break. For
other cases it is the intact native prefix at EOS or the first incomplete
boundary; low successor yield from such late histories is retained as supply
risk rather than repaired. Each call then adds one complete nominated `c` and
generation consumes the full remainder of
the fixed 3084 assistant-output budget. A local witness candidate is only the
immediate first complete free row and must be a strict-IoU nonduplicate of
`h+c`. Full later repeats, malformed rows, cap/EOS and owner losses remain
measured; they do not retroactively erase an otherwise real local transition.

Root visually accepts physical single-owner `c` absence and the local `w`.
The predeclared round-robin priority admits at most 32 packages, with every
first candidate/image ahead of any second candidate. Below 16 accepted packages
the lane stops as supply shortfall and does not train.

For N=16--32, the sole fit has batch2, exactly 32 positive exposures/package,
and 16*N optimizer steps. It uses the inherited Stable50 optimizer and shared
trainer with positive mean2, all admitted conditional-w KL10, retained disjoint
normal-reference KL100 and margin10. There is no dynamicP, QP, negative fit,
dose, seed or coefficient sweep.

## Strongest alternative and decision evidence

The strongest alternative is that conditional local transitions remain
available but the broader fit still harms natural route selection or retained
owners, as the screened single-image fit did. Natural admitted-target recovery,
independent panel owner gains/losses and complete burden decide the endpoint;
teacher loss and forced recovery are separate evidence. Technical invalidity
leaves the scientific question unanswered.

The earlier v1 packets are preserved with zero model calls and superseded by
v2 before launch; the machine v2 packet records the exact delta.
