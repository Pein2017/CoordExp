## Bounded routing assessment

The evidence supports role fit, not a capability leaderboard: Luna-max was
useful for disjoint, mechanically checkable CPU packaging/invariants; Sol-high
was useful for cross-file runtime/trainer integration and real-entry smokes,
but required root-found semantic corrections. The L2 lane receipts and Sol
receipts became decision-bearing only through root replay, cold readback, and
visual/technical acceptance. Because task shape, write surface, verifier, and
acceptance burden differed, these artifacts cannot identify a causal benefit
of “max” effort, Sol versus Luna quality, or adding L2. They also cannot be
used to infer B's outcome while B is running.
