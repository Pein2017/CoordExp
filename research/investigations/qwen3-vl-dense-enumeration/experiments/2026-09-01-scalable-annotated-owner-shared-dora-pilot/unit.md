# Scalable Annotated-Owner Shared DoRA Pilot

## Active contract

> From the immutable four-coordinate `geo_sorted_xy` step-2444 Source, does
> actual-prefix annotated-positive training with fixed preservation in one
> shared, unmerged DoRA adapter improve image-disjoint screen-dev natural-greedy
> annotated-owner coverage over censored teacher-forced transcript learning on
> the identical adapter surface and dose, after a semantic-over-permutation
> cross-image gradient gate and under registered retention and termination
> gates?

This sentence owns the first decision-bearing pilot.  A change to the Source
family, ordering, partial-label semantics, adapter surface, primary contrast,
screen-dev population, acceptance threshold, or stop rule is a phase reset and
requires an amended or fresh unit before execution.

The direction-local code-module contract is preserved at
`probe/human13-output-qp-identity-generalization@a2c049453:openspec/changes/add-annotated-owner-shared-dora-training/proposal.md`;
it is not promoted into canonical `research-probes` by this records-only return.

## Origin and evidence correction

The predecessor result is one shared output-only residual over all thirteen
Human13 images, not thirteen per-image residuals.  It reaches `392 / 392` at
IoU50, IoU60, and IoU80 on that same fixed panel with zero hard debt and
natural EOS.  It establishes finite-panel shared-output compilability only.
Frozen image/prefix states can still let one shared matrix implement a finite
lookup, so the result does not establish held-out transfer, semantic sharing,
ordinary-layer learnability, or population generalization.

The fixed Image2299-authored output payload transfers negatively to the
legacy-12 cohort, but that result is conditional on the Image2299-trained
checkpoint and does not answer whether a shared Human13 residual or a learned
DoRA update transfers from the step-2444 Source.

The user-approved successor changes the intervention surface from an output
residual to ordinary language-block DoRA and changes the outcome from
same-panel overfit to image-disjoint annotated-owner improvement.  Therefore
this is a new unit, not a continuation of the closed Human13 estimand.

### Identifiable outcome

COCO annotations are treated as potentially missing-not-at-random.  The

- annotated owners may supply positive coverage/correction events;

- unmatched generated rows remain `unknown`, not false positives or

## Question, estimand, and claim

### Primary contrast

Both trainable arms start independently from the identical Source, use the
same language-DoRA target set, optimizer family, image presentations,
optimizer steps, effective batch, and evaluation schedule.

- **Censored transcript control (`C`)**: teacher-forced observed annotation
  transcript cross-entropy with only its boundary EOS target masked.
- **Actual-prefix positive treatment (`D0`)**: naturally reached prefixes,
  grouped complete-row annotated-positive correction, fixed source-row
  preservation, annotation-independent exact/near-exact repetition penalty,
  and outer natural-decode acceptance.  No scalar duals, K-FAC/Fisher, CVaR,
  or online parameter server are present.

The unchanged Source is evaluated as a paired reference, but the algorithmic
decision is `D0 - C`, not merely trained adapter versus Source.

### Primary estimand

Let `O_H` be the frozen screen-dev annotated-owner ledger and let
`I_a(o)` indicate strict category-compatible global one-to-one IoU at least
`0.50` under arm `a`'s fresh natural decode.  The decision-owning estimand is

\[
\Delta_{D0-C}^{ann,50}
=
\frac{1}{|O_H|}\sum_{o\in O_H}
\left[I_{D0}(o)-I_C(o)\right].
\]

Report in addition:

- owner-micro and image-macro annotated recall at IoU50, IoU60, and IoU80;
- Source-to-arm and C-to-D0 `0 -> 1` gains, `1 -> 0` losses, and retained owner
  identities, not aggregate recall alone;
- density, size, occlusion, and category strata as diagnostics;
- unmatched-row count and length burden as `unknown`;
- exact repetition, malformed rows, invalid boxes, ordering violations, parser
  failures, cap/nontermination, and three EOS bins: annotated owners remain,
  all annotations covered/unknown, and no EOS before cap; and
- wall time, forwards, backwards, collectives, peak GPU memory, host RSS,
  workers, cache bytes, checkpoint bytes, and final adapter bytes.

The pilot minimum meaningful effect is `D0 - C >= 0.5` percentage point in
owner-micro annotated IoU50, with a paired image-bootstrap 95% confidence
interval lower bound above zero.  A single optimizer seed may reject this
route, but cannot by itself establish an algorithm-level positive claim.

### Permitted positive claim

If all gates pass after paired replication:

> On the frozen image-disjoint COCO train2017 pilot and its observed
> annotated-owner ledger, actual-prefix positive training in one shared
> unmerged DoRA adapter improved strict natural-greedy annotated-owner coverage
> over matched censored transcript learning under the registered recipe.

Not claimed: performance on unannotated objects, unbiased COCO precision or
recall, full-scene completeness, distributional or population generalization,
semantic internalization, architecture promotion, scalable-system efficiency,
production readiness, or superiority of primal-dual optimization.

## Strongest alternative and mathematical discriminator

The strongest alternative is relocated finite lookup or annotation/burden
exploitation: an 18-million-parameter shared adapter can recognize a finite
set of image/prefix states, improve selected annotated owners, and emit more
rows without learning a direction that helps untouched images.

For disjoint train and screen-dev losses `F_T` and `F_H`, define metric
gradients `g_T` and `g_H`.  The trust-normalized train step

\[
\delta_T=-r\frac{G^{-1}g_T}{\lVert g_T\rVert_{G^{-1}}}
\]

satisfies the local held-out bound, when the screen-dev loss has metric Hessian
bound `L_H` along the segment,

\[
F_H(\theta+\delta_T)-F_H(\theta)
\le
-r\frac{g_H^\top G^{-1}g_T}{\lVert g_T\rVert_{G^{-1}}}
+\frac{L_Hr^2}{2}.
\]

Therefore positive cross-image metric-gradient alignment is a falsifiable
local transfer predictor.  It is not a population theorem and the images used
for this screen cannot later be called untouched final validation.

The matched null permutes complete target rows among image/prefix states within
category, row-length, box-size, baseline-deficit, and preconditioned-gradient-
norm bins.  It preserves target-token supply and approximate local difficulty
while breaking the semantic owner/state pairing.  Semantic and null directions
use the same realized-weight metric radius and one registered radius halving at
most.

## Staged outline

### G0: cheap admission and falsification gates

G0 produces diagnostic or local-transfer evidence only; it is not a trained
model-quality result.

1. **Off-panel Human13 residual decode.** Apply the already accepted single
   Human13 shared output residual, without refitting, to a frozen
   density-stratified eight-image off-panel diagnostic.  This directly checks
   whether that residual transfers at all.  A negative is informative about
   the residual, but does not by itself reject language-DoRA learning.
2. **Human13 target-assignment permutation QP.** Run the exact N13
   deficit-matched complete-target permutation null on CPU.  Comparable
   feasibility/norm supports finite interpolation; lower burden for semantic
   pairing supports panel-bounded specificity.  Neither outcome is held-out
   generalization.
3. **Cross-image gradient sentinel.** On the frozen `256 / 128` split, compare
   semantic and matched-permutation trust-normalized directions using
   screen-dev grouped-row margin change and natural Source-owner retention.
   Permit one radius halving only if the registered local-prediction agreement
   fails.
4. **Actual-prefix signal-supply probe.** Before any optimizer step, verify that
   naturally reached prefixes yield at least `64` annotated-positive
   gain/repair events and `64` valid keep events, and record complete-row
   deficits, order eligibility, selected positions, and serialized bank size.

Stop before G1 if semantic screen-dev improvement is nonpositive after the one
allowed radius halving, or if semantic-minus-permutation improvement has a
paired image-bootstrap 95% confidence-interval lower bound at or below zero,
while both directions fit their train events.  Also stop if signal-supply or
mechanics admission fails.

### G1: smallest decision-bearing eight-GPU pilot

- arms: Source evaluation, `C`, and `D0`;
- physical batch: one image per rank;
- world size: eight;
- gradient accumulation: eight microsteps;
- effective global batch: `64` images;
- optimizer steps: `32`;
- fresh natural screen-dev evaluation: steps `0`, `16`, and `32` for both arms;
- D0 natural-prefix bank refresh: Source/step `0` and the accepted step `16`
  state only;
- full vocabulary logits materialized only at selected action positions;
- one fixed optimizer seed for the kill-or-advance pilot; and
- all decision-bearing inference dynamically loads the unmerged adapter over
  the immutable base package.

The research receipt must record actual rather than nominal invocation counts.
No checkpoint is chosen adaptively: step `32` owns the primary comparison
unless a registered safety stop fires earlier.  A failed interval is not
rescued by a different seed, split, loss weight, radius, or checkpoint.

### Conditional successors, not part of G1 execution

- If D0 gains annotated owners but violates the registered preservation gate,

  repetition (`D1`).  D1 is not evidence from this unit until its intervention
  and contrast are separately frozen.
- A broader `20,000` train / `2,000` train-dev, `2,000`-step study is held until
  G1 passes, C and D0 receive paired additional seeds, resource bounds are
  accepted, and a new scale contract is frozen.  COCO `val2017` remains
  untouched until that decision.
- Full-model fine-tuning, K-FAC/Fisher, CVaR, learned exposure counters, and
  per-owner duals are not successors by default.

## Gates and stop rules

### Mechanical invalidity

Any base/package, adapter, split, annotation, prompt, token, geometry, order,
event-bank, evaluator, trainable-surface, DDP denominator, collective-order,
finite-update, adapter-save, fresh-unmerged-reload, or receipt mismatch makes
the affected run `MECHANICAL_INVALID`.  Repair the infrastructure and use a
fresh run identifier; the invalid run remains provenance and supplies no
scientific evidence.

Any decision-bearing reliance on a merged export is also mechanically invalid
for this unit.

### Scientific stops

- **No semantic-over-permutation cross-image gradient:** stop before training.
- **Insufficient actual-prefix signal:** fewer than `64` gain/repair events or
  `64` keep events stops G1.
- **No meaningful natural-decode advantage:** at step 32,
  `D0 - C < 0.5` percentage point annotated IoU50, or paired-bootstrap lower
  bound at or below zero, stops scale-up.
- **Margin-only change:** teacher-forced/grouped margins rise but D0 natural
  greedy does not beat C by `0.5` point; stop the route.
- **Retention failure:** D0 retains less than `98.5%` of Source-covered
  screen-dev annotated owners, loses more Source owners than C, or rotates
  losses across intervals while aggregate retention passes; do not claim
  preservation or scale.
- **Termination failure:** D0 cap/nontermination count exceeds twice C; stop.
- **Unknown-output burden:** unmatched-row burden growing more than `25%`
  triggers blinded complete/manual audit and blocks any precision or
  hallucination interpretation; it is not automatically a hallucination
  failure.
- **One-seed positive:** a passing first seed authorizes paired replication of
  C and D0 only.  It does not authorize a positive algorithm claim or G2 by
  itself.

Stop after the registered G1 verdict.  Do not continue searching merely
because a stronger mechanism or larger study is imaginable.

## Alignment and preservation

- **Intervention surface:** ordinary language-block DoRA parameters.
- **Proxy observations:** grouped-row margins and cross-image gradient
  alignment.
- **Final evaluation surface:** fresh-cold original-prompt natural greedy,
  strict annotated-owner one-to-one matching.
- **Required transfer:** local semantic alignment must predict natural
  screen-dev behavior better than the matched permutation null.
- **Preserved behavior:** Source-covered annotated owners, valid row grammar,
  declared order, parser validity, bounded output, and natural termination.
- **Neutral evidence:** unmatched outputs, unexecuted rows, missing annotations,
  mechanically invalid cases, and post-annotation EOS correctness.
- **Signal supply:** G0 event counts and deficit distributions decide whether
  the selected intervention has enough correctly attributed positive and keep
  events to justify G1.
