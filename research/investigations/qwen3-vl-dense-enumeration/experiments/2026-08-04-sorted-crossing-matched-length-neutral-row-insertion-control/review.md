## Post-execution independent audit

The final immutable artifact received a second independent Fable-5 review.
Verdict: **qualified pass for unit closure**.

The reviewer independently reconstructed all source and output seals, the
`9/12/5` plan split, all `54` requests, the `0`-quarantine ledger, gates one
through five, the failed specificity gate, route arithmetic, and every
non-voting sensitivity. Image `2299` is absent, the same-run float32 replay is
fresh rather than circular self-comparison, and gate-six precedence is applied
correctly.

The audit identified one conclusion-shaping post-hoc covariate: neutral-row
materiality is almost predicted by the number of native rows jumped backward.
All seven nonmaterial controls have `rows_back_distance <= 3`, while thirteen
of fourteen material controls have `rows_back_distance >= 5`; the single
exception is `gt:4134:29`. Spearman correlation with relative coordinate delta
is `-0.7510843921476228`. This narrows the interpretation to a failed neutral
control and makes sorted-route regression the strongest alternative. It does
not turn that post-hoc association into the unit's causal result.

A subsequent CPU-only feasibility check found an admissible
`rows_back_distance <= 3` candidate for only `5/12` specificity owners and
`5/9` voting owners. That is insufficient to rebuild the specificity gate
without changing frozen predicates. The review therefore agrees to close this
insertion-control line without another GPU capture.
