# Static RLOO owner-coverage one-update vertical

Executed result: [results.md](results.md).  The registered IoU50 owner-count
decision is **`NO_TRAIN_GAIN_STATIC_RLOO_STOP`** (`53 / 67 -> 53 / 67`), while

## Frozen question and decision

> From frozen C, does one static K4 RLOO update on eight registered training
> images increase natural-greedy IoU50 annotated-owner coverage on those same
> eight images relative to C?

This unit optimizes **train behavior first**.  Any positive net train-panel
change is retained as bounded train-positive evidence, even if diagnostic debt
or image-disjoint metrics do not improve.  Train plus image-disjoint evaluation
improvement would be stronger successor evidence, but it is not a launch gate
for this unit.

The primary comparison is the frozen C greedy anchor versus one cold-loaded
candidate under the same prompt, images, tokenizer, precision, generation
policy, owner ledger, and category-consistent global one-to-one IoU50 matcher.
IoU60/80, duplicates, unmatched valid rows, invalid/dropped rows, natural EOS,
caps, length, and natural ordering are monitors.  Unmatched valid rows remain
unknown and natural disorder is legal.

- **`TRAIN_POSITIVE`**: candidate total IoU50 annotated-owner coverage is at
  least one owner higher than C on the eight images.  Keep and report the
  adapter; authorize a separately recorded wider train-248 and image-disjoint
  read.  This does not establish generalization.
- **`NO_TRAIN_GAIN_STATIC_RLOO_STOP`**: candidate total is not higher.  Record
  the finite result and stop this fixed one-step candidate without an in-unit
  seed or learning-rate sweep.  This does not reject policy gradient or shared
  DoRA generally.
- **`MECHANICAL_INVALID`**: an identity, reconstruction, finite-value,
  collective, persistence, or cold-read invariant fails.  Publish no
  scientific candidate; repair mechanics and restart from immutable C under a
  new attempt.

## Intervention

For image `i` and frozen seed `k`, let `S_ik` be the IoU50 matched owner set,
`O_i` its annotated-owner set, and `r_ik = |S_ik| / |O_i|`.  With `K=4`, use

```text
A_ik = K / (K - 1) * (r_ik - mean_k(r_i))
L_i  = -(1 / K) * sum_k A_ik * sum_t log pi_C(a_ikt | h_ikt).
```

Append the tokenizer-verified terminal `im_end` token `151645` to each stored
body and score every action token.  Advantages are detached and must sum to
zero per image.  No length normalization, whitening, advantage clipping,
PPO ratio, critic, reference-model term, QP projection, or online resampling is
allowed.

Use one image per rank under eight-rank replicated DDP.  The first three local
backwards use `no_sync`; the fourth synchronizes.  Local division by four and
DDP rank averaging implement the global mean over 32 trajectories.  Keep the
model in evaluation mode on the exact FP32/SDPA behavior surface.  Train only
the registered 588 language-DoRA tensors (18,006,016 scalars); freeze base,
vision, aligner, selected embedding delta, tied embeddings, and output head.

Apply one fresh AdamW step with learning rate `2.5e-6`, zero weight decay, no
scheduler, and global gradient-norm clipping at one.  The attempt budget is
exactly one scientific update.  Persist only the unmerged shared DoRA adapter;
there is no durable merged checkpoint, per-image payload, or external memory.
