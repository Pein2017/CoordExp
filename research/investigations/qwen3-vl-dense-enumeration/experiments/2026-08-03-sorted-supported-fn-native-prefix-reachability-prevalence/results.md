# Results

## Verdict

The unit is complete and independently verified.

For the frozen twelve-image panel, the `114` native false-negative owners that
already possess tested category-conditioned localization support do not look
like a primarily STOP-gated cohort:

- continuation is favored at some usable support context for `113/114`;
- the target category reaches top three for `106/114`, but rank one for only
  `67/114`;
- the target physical owner reaches rank one within its same-category group
  for `72/114`; and
- all four favorable conditions occur before or at the sorted frontier for
  `49/114` under the primary U bound and `48/114` under the L sensitivity.

The simple covered-object-ledger explanation is also demoted. Among the `42`
owners that never reach physical-owner rank one, an uncovered other owner is
the best same-category competitor for `38/42`, while a covered other owner
appears for `9/42`; these predicates overlap. Only four owners are explained
exclusively by covered-other competition, whereas 33 are explained
exclusively by uncovered-other competition.

The bounded conclusion is:

> Existing owner-local visual and geometry support is common in the native
> false-negative cohort, but natural enumeration still depends on when the
> owner becomes due and whether the row-composition path can release and
> realize that owner against other still-uncovered owners. STOP and a pure
> covered-set memory failure are not the leading simple explanations for this
> cohort.

This is a post-hoc descriptive result. It selects one prospective
crossing-boundary discriminator; it does not estimate natural row probability,
identify a causal module, or promote training or architecture.

## Positive-reference calibration

At the exact due boundary of the `141` native true-positive owners:

- due-context localization support is present for `130/141`;
- gate open, category rank one, and category top three are each `141/141`;
- physical-owner rank one is `121/141`; and
- favorable-top-three-before/at is `102/141`.

The positive reference validates the channel ladder as a useful descriptor,
but it is not a matched causal control. The FN arm uses an optimistic
any-context operator, whereas the TP arm uses exactly one due context.

## What the descriptive census rules out or demotes

### STOP as the primary cause

Only one of the 114 owners never has an open gate at a usable support context.
This does not prove that natural continuation is always calibrated, but it
rules out STOP as the dominant explanation for the majority supported-FN
cohort.

### A pure covered-owner ledger failure

Of the 42 owners that never become the best same-category physical owner,
uncovered-other competition is much more common than covered-other
competition. Thirty-six of those 42 are `person`, and 27 occur in images
`16228` or `7511`. The observed pattern is therefore closer to dense
remaining-owner ranking and crowding than to a simple failure to remember
which owner was already emitted.

### Frontier position as a hard emission barrier

Sixteen FN owners have support only after the sorted frontier, but 21 of 141
native true positives are themselves emitted after the frontier has passed.
The frontier is a useful timing coordinate, not an eligibility mask.

## Critical interpretation correction

The `49/114` favorable-before/at result is not a cohort of 49 owners that were
already due but failed release. Across these owners, every favorable context
is root or ahead of frontier; none is exactly at frontier. A separate exact
crossing-boundary census finds:

- `26` U-bound owners favorable at their crossing boundary;
- `25` L-bound owners favorable at their crossing boundary;
- `24` favorable under both bounds at the same crossing context;
- `12/26` whose exact next native row strict-matches a physical owner; and
- `14/26` whose exact next native row is unmatched.

The full 26-owner U cohort owns the next prospective probe. Restricting to the
12 clean matched rows would select away the other half of the cohort and bias the
probe toward valid-owner displacement.

Two further caveats remain descriptive but important:

- any-context prevalence rises with the number of observed support contexts,
  so owner-level rates partly reflect exposure opportunity; and
- owner rank one can be trivial when the same-category population has one
  member. Six of the 49 favorable owners are favorable only through such a
  singleton group.

## Observed

- Local category-conditioned localization support coexists with native
  omission for a majority of eligible false negatives.
- The natural continuation gate is open almost everywhere in this supported
  cohort.
- Same-category owner competition, especially from uncovered owners, remains
  common in dense `person` scenes.
- A material cross-image subset is favorable at the exact boundary where the
  sorted route crosses the owner.

## Supported

- A prospective crossing-boundary decomposition is warranted.
- The next probe should separate natural description release from
  owner-conditioned coordinate realization and displacement.
- The unmatched next-row stratum must remain in the primary cohort.

## Not supported

- No claim that 49 owners were naturally ready to emit at their observed

- No natural row probability obtained by multiplying the separately scored

- No causal estimate comparing FN and TP cohorts.
- No population prevalence beyond the frozen twelve images.
- No claim that the prefix implements or lacks a set ledger.
- No owner-commit token, typed binding, InfoNCE, slot, detector, RL objective,
  or other architecture promotion.

## Next discriminator

Run one prospective score-only crossing-boundary probe over all 26 U-bound
owners. For each owner, compare the exact native self-prefix immediately
before and after the row that crosses the owner, then decompose the target
natural row into:

1. natural description-path release; and
2. coordinates under the exact target description.

Classify the observed failure as release-lost, owner-conditioned realization
failure, or displacement to another physical owner. Treat starvation only as
an existing-trajectory tag. Add no free rollout initially; low-temperature
coordinate sampling is permitted only if likelihood and deterministic greedy
coordinates leave the branch unresolved.

The parallel owner-commit binding design remains a complexity ceiling and a
possible later treatment. Current evidence does not require it.
