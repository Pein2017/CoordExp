# Results

## Verdict

Image `2299` has now been added as a separate prospective slice to the full
mechanism chain. It does not change any completed legacy-twelve denominator.

The exact native rollout over the refined `46`-owner image (`38 person`,
`8 tie`) emits `24` valid prediction rows and stops naturally after `216`
assistant tokens plus `im_end`. Strict one-to-one matching gives:

- `19` true-positive owners;
- `27` false-negative owners;
- `5` false-positive or unmatched rows; and
- zero matcher ambiguity classes.

The prospective owner-accessibility arm then does not meet its preregistered
calibration-transfer validity gate. Only `14/19` native true-positive controls
(`73.7%`) clear the frozen legacy support rule at their exact native due
boundary, below the required `80%`. Consequently:

> No valid image-2299 fraction of "visually recoverable" or
> `resolved_tested_localization_support` false negatives is reported. All
> `27` native false negatives have the primary disposition
> `withheld_calibration_nontransfer`.

Applying the frozen threshold mechanically gives `16/27` apparently resolved
and `11/27` apparently persistent cases, but these numbers are
**descriptive sensitivity only**. They are not a `59.3%` recoverability
estimate. The same rule has just failed on five owners the model actually
emitted, so using it to classify omitted owners would overstate precision.
The preregistered scale stress test is therefore **void, not negative**.

This is a scientifically useful instrument-validity failure. Image `2299`
contains large, human-visible, densely packed same-category owners. The
positive-control floor was not met, so the legacy pooled functional is not
validated for classifying this image's false negatives. That does not by
itself prove a distribution shift: sealed legacy images have substantial
per-image control-rate variation. The result nevertheless reinforces that the
old persistent cohort is not a direct measure of frozen visual-tower absence.
It does not invalidate the old twelve-image census, whose calibration and
confirmation were internal to its frozen panel.

No training method, architecture, slot, owner head, image-resolution change,
or threshold retuning is promoted.

```text
under one canonical category query,
one frozen owner-centred candidate bank,
and tested native self-prefix contexts
```

## Why the transfer-validity gate was not met

Five native true positives fail the frozen due-boundary support conjunction.
The failures are not uniform evidence of weak vision:

- three `person` controls have concentrated local landscapes but insufficient
  frozen `peak_lift` under the much larger same-category candidate field;
- two `tie` controls include an exact owner-rank-one candidate and strong
  continuation margin but miss the pooled concentration criterion; and
- all five are owners the natural decoder actually emitted and strict-matched.

This rules out reading the five failures as five visually unavailable true
positives. It does not uniquely establish a new image-level non-invariance:
under the same reconstructed rule, sealed legacy per-image TP-control rates
range from `65%` to `100%`, and `3/12` legacy images also fall below `80%`.
Ordinary image-level variation around the pooled calibration is therefore the
strongest alternative to a candidate-population shift.

A category-only control sensitivity is reportable but cannot repair the
aggregate gate: `13/16` person controls pass (`81.25%`), while `1/3` tie
controls pass. The person result clears the floor by only one owner, and the
legacy discovery calibration contained zero tie controls. The tie interface
is category-incomplete rather than demonstrated to have shifted. Selecting
the person-only result after observing the aggregate failure would change the
estimand.

## Descriptive sensitivity, explicitly non-primary

The sealed analyzer also records what the frozen rule would say if applied
mechanically despite the unmet validity gate. These are raw diagnostic counts,
not interpretable FN dispositions and not a recoverability rate:

| Category | Raw frozen-rule resolved | Raw frozen-rule persistent |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| person | `14/22` | `8/22` |
| tie | `2/5` | `3/5` |
| all | `16/27` | `11/27` |

There are no ambiguity-bound flips in this mechanical application. This table
must not be used to claim that `14/22` persons or `2/5` ties are visually
recoverable. For debugging the instrument only, the raw persistent rows are
not confined to tiny boxes:

- median minimum dimension is about `105 px` for the descriptive persistent
  group and `115.5 px` for the descriptive resolved group;
- median normalized area is about `0.0239` versus `0.0252`; and
- `22/27` native misses have minimum dimension at least `64` pixels
  (`19` in `64..127`, `3` at least `128`).

This raw pattern sharply differs from the legacy-twelve post-confirmation
scale signal,
where persistent owners had median minimum dimension `17.5 px` versus `30 px`
for resolved owners. Because the primary image-2299 classifier is invalid,
this contrast is hypothesis-generating only. Still, it is enough to reject the
idea that apparent size alone can safely label the image's misses as
vision-limited.

The free greedy category-query sidecar is an even narrower diagnostic. It
strictly recovers all `19/19` native-TP owners at some tested context, but only
`1/27` native false negatives (`1/22 person`, `0/5 tie`). This means the
unconstrained greedy winner rarely lands on omitted owners. It does **not**
mean the other `26` have no visual information: a single greedy winner tests
mode selection, not the full owner-local likelihood landscape.

The root-only position screen avoids using post-row `t`, because the sorted
target order is `(y1, x1)` and therefore confounds owner position with due-row
index after generation begins.

The prospective frozen-root-support counts (`4/46` under L, `5/46` under U)
inherit the unmet transfer-validity gate and are descriptive only. The native
TP/FN spatial screen below does not use that support threshold and is therefore

The single image has a mild bottom-right descriptive gradient, but its

The unchanged legacy-twelve screen does not reproduce a stable bottom-right

centre-y `-0.162`, raster distance `+0.156`, and area `-0.529`. Position is
therefore not promoted as a RoPE, raster-alignment, or causal omission
mechanism. Apparent scale remains the stronger legacy association.

## Relation to earlier image-2299 rounds

This result complements rather than replaces the earlier image-specific
mechanism probes:

1. The historical random-versus-sorted screen found that the sorted adapter's
   next owner changes with the appended row, while the random adapter collapses
   toward a stable preferred person. Random order did not create a useful
   order-free ledger.
2. The near-complete human-relabel successor probe found `93/96` uniquely
   matched immediate successors and zero immediate self repeats across four
   appended people. This is strong local commit-like transition evidence, but
   remains compatible with last-row spatial routing.
3. Same-covered-set order experiments showed both an order-robust local
   covered-owner suppression case and order-sensitive next-owner changes.
   The state is hybrid and route-shaped, not a clean set-only ledger.
4. The current native rollout still misses `27/46` refined owners despite
   reaching the bottom rows and stopping naturally. Its misses are interleaved
   with hits rather than being a simple suffix truncated by the token horizon.

Together, these rounds support a model in which image `2299` contains strong
usable local owner-routing signals, but a highly competitive same-category
field and prefix-conditioned transition policy do not compose into exhaustive
one-pass coverage. The present unit cannot estimate how many of the 27 misses
belong to that trainable routing regime because its imported support readout
failed transfer.

## Observed

- Native strict recall is `19/46`; the model naturally stops after `24` rows.
- The old owner-accessibility transfer-validity floor is not met on this
  ultra-dense image.
- The failed controls include owners that the model successfully emitted.
- The frozen threshold's descriptive FN split is `16/11`, but it is not a
  valid prevalence estimate.
- Misses are not concentrated only among tiny objects or only at one image
  corner.
- No supported-FN reachability or crossing denominator is validly opened.

## Supported

- The legacy-twelve `114/202` result remains evidence that many false
  negatives are not a single visual-absence class.
- Accessibility interfaces need positive-control transfer validation before
  their rates are applied to a new scene distribution.
- Image `2299` is an ultra-dense same-category stress regime for which the
  pooled legacy rule is not validated for FN classification; ordinary
  per-image variation remains an alternative explanation.
- Sorted prefix state has owner-sensitive transition capacity, but that does
  not imply exhaustive set completion.

## Ruled out or demoted

- A valid image-2299 statement that `16/27` misses are visually recoverable.
- A valid image-2299 statement that the other `11/27` are visually unavailable.
- Simple max-token truncation as the native failure.
- A stable bottom-right or raster-end position effect across the thirteen
  images.
- Apparent owner size as a sufficient classifier for this image.

## Unresolved

- Which image-2299 misses retain usable fixed-resolution visual information
  under a calibration/readout that is invariant to category count and
  candidate-population size.
- Whether a category-aware or candidate-population-normalized functional can
  pass a genuinely held-out positive-control transfer test without post-hoc
  threshold rescue.
- How much of image-2299 omission is owner proposal, same-category ranking,
  extent/geometry drift, or prefix-conditioned route scheduling.
- Whether a simple training-side intervention can increase gained owners while
  retaining the 19 current native owners.

## Stop decision and next discriminator

This prospective unit stops at the calibration gate. It must not retune on
image `2299`, widen the bank after reading its misses, or run S3 on the raw
`16` descriptive cases.

The smallest immediate successor is the completed CPU-only placement of
`14/19` inside the sealed legacy per-image TP-control distribution, plus the
five-control failure-mode decomposition. If a decision-bearing image-2299
support estimate is still needed after that context, it requires a
**separately preregistered transfer study**, not a threshold repair on this
image. Candidate-population normalization or category-aware calibration may
be compared on calibration images, but the chosen rule must be frozen before
held-out dense same-category images and must pass positive-control retention.
Only such a readout may estimate the fraction of image-2299 misses with tested
localization support.

This is a measurement repair, not an architecture proposal. The existing
training-design discussion may still target owner selection and route
preservation using the valid legacy-twelve mechanism evidence, but it must not
use the image-2299 `16/27` sensitivity split as an admission label.
