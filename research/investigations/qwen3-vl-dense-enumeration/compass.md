## Cross-cutting metric boundaries

- Annotated-owner counts are category-consistent global one-to-one matches at
  named IoU thresholds. Summing thresholds counts the same owner more than
  once.
- Unmatched predictions are annotation-relative under incomplete labels. They
  are not automatically hallucinations.
- Entity/category correctness and geometry correctness are separate review
  axes; a repeat candidate is not automatically a semantic duplicate.
- Forced or crossed continuations are conditional interventions, not native
  deployment scores.
- Technical invalidity, missing null arms, and CPU-only preparation are kept
  separate from scientific negative results.

## Unresolved scientific questions

- Which owner-coverage objective can improve natural complete-output owner sets
  while preserving Source owners and generation burden on an independent
  evaluation population?
- Does semantic shared-output compression persist beyond the valid train-only
  N4/N8/N16 ladder once a complete, matched N32 null contrast exists?
- Can local row-state or layer-direction effects survive native prompt
  generation without being reduced to category correction, geometry changes,
  global assignment, or checkpoint-specific route compatibility?
- Under incomplete annotations, which apparent unmatched or duplicate changes
  survive separate entity/category and geometry adjudication?
