# Qwen3 Vision-Language Autoregressive Detection Research Compass

This is the current program-level synthesis. Executed facts remain owned by
the linked experiment records. The synthesis separates finite-panel fitting,
natural owner coverage, causal fixed-prefix evidence, and incomplete or
technical execution. It does not promote an architecture or a production
checkpoint.

## Current questions and evidence

### Can the model fit a dense fixed panel through different parameter surfaces?

Human13 contains nested N=2, N=4, and N=13 panels with 65, 123, and 392
annotated owners respectively. The shared output-QP and magnitude-only DoRA
routes each achieve every owner in the applicable panel at each of IoU50,
IoU60, and IoU80 under RP1.0, with natural EOS and no hard debt. These are
finite-panel programmability results from the exact Source checkpoint. They do
not establish held-out transfer, semantic sharing, or population
generalization.

- [Human13 magnitude-only result](experiments/2026-09-01-human13-dora-magnitude-finite-overfit/results.md)

The image-disjoint Direct C-D0 screen is a separate owner-preservation

### Which natural owner-coverage objectives survive their controls?

The broad-support refreshed RLOO update is positive on its train-248
owner-count vector. A fresh paired soft-owner QP update is also positive versus
its anchor but trails ordinary refreshed RLOO by three summed threshold hits.
The result is train-side natural-greedy evidence only; validation,
repeatability, and generalization remain unestablished.

### What does crossing the first divergent row identify?

The Source/Rweak four-cell study is a completed, outcome-stratified finite
panel. It identifies row, checkpoint, and interaction contrasts while keeping
direct action changes, global matching, burden, and suffix attribution
explicit. It does not identify a training gradient, a transferable owner-state
mechanism, or a population frequency. Its separate model-routing benchmark is
operational evidence and remains outside this scientific synthesis.

The antecedent full512 reference has Source/Rweak IoU50 owner counts of
`2225/2310` out of `3759` annotated owners. The selected 32-image panel is
outcome-stratified (`16` Source-loss, `8` gain-only, `8` equal-owner-set token
changed) and is not a population estimate; its local ordering must not be
extended to the full reference. The no-net statement above applies specifically
to the autonomous owner-outcome arms, not to every Source-versus-Rweak route.

## Cross-cutting metric boundaries

- Annotated-owner counts are category-consistent global one-to-one matches at
  named IoU thresholds. Summing thresholds counts the same owner more than
  once.
- Unmatched predictions are annotation-relative under incomplete labels. They
  are not automatically hallucinations.
- Entity/category correctness and geometry correctness are separate review
  axes; a repeat candidate is not automatically a semantic duplicate.
- Forced or crossed continuations are conditional interventions, not native
  deployment scores.
- Technical invalidity, missing null arms, and CPU-only preparation are kept
  separate from scientific negative results.

## Unresolved scientific questions

- Which owner-coverage objective can improve natural complete-output owner sets
  while preserving Source owners and generation burden on an independent
  evaluation population?
- Does semantic shared-output compression persist beyond the valid train-only
  N4/N8/N16 ladder once a complete, matched N32 null contrast exists?
- Can local row-state or layer-direction effects survive native prompt
  generation without being reduced to category correction, geometry changes,
  global assignment, or checkpoint-specific route compatibility?
- Under incomplete annotations, which apparent unmatched or duplicate changes
  survive separate entity/category and geometry adjudication?

No architecture, decoder change, or production checkpoint is promoted by this
compass. Package ownership and implementation status belong to the active
OpenSpec/docs route, not to this scientific synthesis.
