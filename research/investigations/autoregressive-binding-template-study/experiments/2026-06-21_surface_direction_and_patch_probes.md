# June 21 Surface Direction And Patch Probes

This phase absorbs 23 records covering adapter/surface decompositions, coordinate target directions, local simplex surfaces, route ridges, and post-box hidden-state patches.

## Read

The phase tests whether coordinate-basin centering can be steered by output-embedding directions and whether post-box state carries descriptor, coordinate, or route-relevant information. The recurring result is a split between signals that are readable, signals that are causally movable, and signals that survive continuation.

## Evidence Scope

Tiny and selected-panel causal patch evidence. Useful for mechanism design; insufficient as population evidence.
