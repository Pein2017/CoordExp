# Coordinate Basin And Binding State

The absorbed records repeatedly distinguish coordinate-token probability repair from object-binding repair. A coordinate basin can be readable and movable while the selected instance, current box, or next-row cursor remains wrong.

Use this as a local mechanism: coordinate-basin evidence is necessary but not sufficient for claiming instance binding repair.
