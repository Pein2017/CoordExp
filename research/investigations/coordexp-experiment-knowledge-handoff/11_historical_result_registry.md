## Registry policy

Rows must carry checkpoint, config, dataset/slice, decode, evaluator, artifact,
and denominator identity. If any one is unavailable, mark the row `unverified`
and do not place it in a ranked table. New rows should record whether the value
is official COCO, a proxy view, F1-ish diagnostic matching, Oracle-K support, or
a smoke/receipt metric.
