## Extraction coverage caveat

1,142 marked `needs-summary`, 20 `needs-adjudication`, and the newest
2026-08-28/29 Image2299 sessions outside that frozen corpus. This packet uses
canonical records plus bounded session context; it does not claim complete
historical byte-level authorship.
