# Qwen3-VL dense enumeration

## Session-backed question

The dense-enumeration sessions treated the problem as a sequence of falsifiable
behavior probes: first establish admission and owner-set evidence, then test
spatial scope, image breadth, source-route preservation, and finally
prefix-local/on-policy treatments. The user-facing stop rule was to avoid
promoting a treatment without held-out owner gain, duplicate/length controls,
and a bounded transfer result.
