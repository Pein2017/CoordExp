# June 10-12 FN Guidance And Coordinate Basins

This summary absorbs 23 raw source records focused on false-negative recovery, same-slot prefix-state lock, context-band behavior, and coordinate-basin accessibility.

## Read

The key result is that some false negatives are conditionally recoverable under coordinate or hidden-state guidance. The vase case is the cleanest same-slot prefix-state lock example; book and chair rows are harder and push toward context-band or same-description disambiguation rather than single-token visual patching.

## Evidence Scope

Case-study and selected-panel evidence. Wrong-control coordinate guidance remains a leakage risk and must be preserved in future decode panels.
