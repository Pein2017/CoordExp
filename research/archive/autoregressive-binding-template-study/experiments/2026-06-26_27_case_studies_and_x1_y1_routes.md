# June 26-27 Case Studies And X1/Y1 Routes

This phase absorbs 17 case-study records focused on prefix history, contextual tail binding, staged prefix patches, and x1-to-y1 route or basin behavior.

## Read

The most concrete finding is that x1 can behave like a key into later y-coordinate behavior in selected cases, but the train/val split is sharp. Some train rows keep target-y readability after forced x1, while val rows can remain locked in a lower-y basin despite the same scaffold. The records therefore support route and basin hypotheses, not a universal coordinate-key rule.

## Evidence Scope

Case studies and selected route/readout sweeps over prefix-denoising checkpoints. These are high-value diagnostic records, not population metrics.
