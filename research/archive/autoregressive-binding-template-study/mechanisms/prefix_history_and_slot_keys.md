# Prefix History And Slot Keys

The later branch records show that prefix history and forced coordinate slots can act like keys into subsequent coordinate basins in selected cases. The effect is not uniform: train and validation rows can diverge sharply under similar scaffolds.

Treat forced-slot probes as case-level evidence until validated over a broader, stratified panel.
