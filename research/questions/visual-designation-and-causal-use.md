# Visual designation and causal use

**Question:** can a useful object-specific signal be generated from the clean input and actually consumed to produce a coherent row, rather than merely being readable or causing generic continuation? This page owns the durable painted-cue and early binding lineage. It does not revive its old architectural or execution rules.

## From a mark to an internal actuator

The [painted preparation](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-08-pvci-step0-preparation-results/unit.md) and [pre-commit probes](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-08-pvci-step1-precommit-probes/unit.md) showed strong steering by explicit image marks, including held-out rows. Tight boundaries outperformed coarse/center-only hints. Tight wrong-object marks alone could not separate object identity from copying the drawn box.

[Anti-copy training](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-08-pvci-step2-anticopy-snap-radius-results/unit.md) partially changed this into sketch-and-snap behavior. [Corrupted wrong-object controls](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-09-pvci-corrupted-wrong-object-row-binding/unit.md) then separated the scheduled target, source object and corrupted mark geometry: the anti-copy model recovered more coherent source-object rows rather than simply copying the mark. This supports bounded row binding, not geometry-invariant identity or ordinary detector improvement. The separate [identity-conflict sanity check](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-09-pvci-identity-conflict/unit.md) showed severe loss of ordinary one-shot enumeration after row specialization.

The [stored-feature delta](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-09-pvci-feature-store-delta-cursor/unit.md) reproduced the same-row effect without a painted vision forward at generation. This does not remove the painted information used to build the stored delta. [Component localization](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-09-pvci-visual-feature-component-localization/unit.md) found the primary image-embedding path much stronger than DeepStack-only deltas in that setup. [Region splitting](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-09-pvci-region-split-visual-delta-cursor/unit.md) favored a mark neighborhood plus distributed context, not an exclusively local mark or object box. Coarse pixel-to-token masks remain an alternative explanation for apparent spatial spread.

[Cross-image additive reuse](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-09-pvci-cross-image-delta-transfer/unit.md) did not establish a reusable cursor; exact visual-feature signature requirements also limited eligibility. By contrast, [same-object photometric transforms](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-09-pvci-same-object-aug-delta-bridge/unit.md) preserved substantial steering. The signal was not just exact pixel-cache identity, but geometric-transform robustness and image-disjoint endogenous control remained open.

## A causal state still needs to be produced and used

[Post-scatter equivalence](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-09-pvci-post-scatter-image-token-equivalence/unit.md) and [residual-boundary decomposition](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-09-pvci-layer-boundary-residual-decomposition/unit.md) established an internal entry and immediate next-token causal handle on the selected strict replay events. Patching a raw attention/MLP branch was not equivalent to replacing the accumulated residual state. Tested layer bands, immediate tokens and selected cases are not universal layer ownership or full-row guarantees.

The [four-event sequential schedule](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-10-pvci-sequential-control-variable-causal-schedule/unit.md) separated continuation, description and geometry interventions. Persistent visual conditioning was a ceiling, not necessary for every selected success. [Endogenous synthesis](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-10-pvci-endogenous-boundary-state-synthesis/unit.md) found exact additive deltas effective but pooled-feature linear maps ineffective on its tiny image-disjoint panel. Low-rank oracle reconstruction itself lost signal, so neither predictor inadequacy nor nonlinear capacity alone was identified. Structured token-space rendering remains a proposal, not a discovered module.

Most decisively, the [320-image held-out representation gate](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-11-pvci-causal-proposal-bridge/heldout-representation-results-2026-07-12.md) found target-specific proposal information beyond its controls. The separate [own-prefix behavioral panel](../../docs/history/research-records/2026-09-15-root-collapse/sources/research/ideas/qwen3-vl-painted-gt-transcription-probe/experiments/2026-07-11-pvci-causal-proposal-bridge/own-prefix-causal-behavior-results-2026-07-12.md) failed: slightly higher annotated recall accompanied large duplicate, malformed and closure debts; wrong-image and token-permuted feedback reproduced much of the harmful signature. Norm-matched random was a null there, not proof that all generic controls are null in later checkpoints. Gradient/feedback attestations supported actual wiring, not target-specific consumption.

## What survives retirement

The reusable decomposition is: privileged information → exact internal actuator → endogenous synthesis → target-specific consumption → full natural consequence. A success at one arrow does not certify the next. Keep the representations as research handles, and the failed delivery routes as counterexamples. Old fixed gates, default model routing and proposed slots do not become current rules.

Reopening is justified by a new discriminating contrast: correct versus source-swapped control on the same legal row boundary, followed by ordinary-parameter original-input realization when deployment is claimed. A spatial renderer, separate adapter, or altered lifetime must beat an appropriate existing comparator at the actual outcome; a bigger bridge or longer rollout is not automatically the next experiment. [Alternatives](../alternatives.md) keeps these unanswered branches visible; [preservation](preservation-and-credit.md) and [history](history-repetition-stopping.md) own their full-policy obligations.
