# Preservation and credit: a new owner can displace an old one

## Question and evidence unit

The estimand is the joint vector of newly gained physical owners, lost source owners and hard output debt under a matched dose/population. Net count alone is insufficient. KL, surrogate credit and literal repetition are not physical-owner retention measures. See [model/data identities](../assets.md).

**Question:** does the update improve the declared final owner set and burden, not merely the target token or a local proxy? Gains, losses and retained identities must be visible separately; a net score hides owner exchange.

## Evidence chain

Single-route imitation (catalog:2026-07-21-best-sampled-trajectory-positive-row-imitation-screen) added selected owners but lost ordinary ones. Multiple-route/source-preserving training (catalog:2026-07-22-source-route-preservation-and-multiple-sampled-route-training-screen) was safer in parts of its finite panel, not uniformly safe outside treated images. Fixed-dose image breadth (catalog:2026-07-22-constant-dose-image-breadth-treatment-screen) did not produce its hoped-for held-out advantage. These are contrasts with specific exposure and decode conditions, not a prohibition on data breadth or CE.

Direct C-D0 (catalog:2026-09-01-annotated-owner-direct-c-d0-pilot) stopped its frozen bundle because preservation and quality predicates failed. The separate soft-owner QP versus refreshed RLOO (catalog:2026-09-03-iterative-soft-owner-qp-vs-rloo-vertical) did not establish useful rescue of a realized owner loss. Their anchors and populations differ; their numbers must not be pooled.

The KL microscope (catalog:2026-09-11-greedy-preservation-microscope) found small average KL alongside many protected argmax flips. The margin-aware fit (catalog:2026-09-11-margin-preserved-positive-branch) reduced eligible flips and improved the aggregate, but did not satisfy every per-case repair/continuation condition. Positive-progress matching (catalog:2026-09-11-positive-progress-matched-control) weakened a scalar-dose explanation while exposing reference-set concentration. Margin flips are not themselves owner-error labels.

Earlier [row-specialization evidence](preservation-and-credit.md) supplies the same warning from a different route: strong visual designation and anti-copy row behavior coexisted with severe ordinary one-shot degradation. Its suggestion of a separate routed/composed adapter is retained as an unproven architectural alternative, not a current requirement or evidence that the present ordinary-parameter training task cannot hold both abilities. [Visual causal use](visual-designation-and-causal-use.md) owns the measured prehistory.

The closed P/R16 ranking repair (catalog:2026-09-16-source256-output-ranking-repair) supplies a later identity-level counterexample: R retained59 of91 starting gained owners, losing32 while adding31 replacements, despite better local FN. Repeat proxy rose from P's263 to R's659. A nearly unchanged gain total is not preservation of the same gained identities. Even the inference-only fresh128 norm policy (catalog:2026-09-17-readout-norm-fresh128) has G30/L16 behind net+14; no-parameter-change does not imply no owner loss.

## Negative credit has two independent traps

Positive versus repeat-event learning (catalog:2026-09-11-positive-branch-vs-repeat-event) collected no eligible sampled negatives, yielding identical weights. That is a supply failure for the contrast, not evidence that the penalty is ineffective. Stable50 geometric dedup (catalog:2026-09-11-stable50-geometric-dedup) did change output, but reduced its repeat proxy while increasing invalid geometry. Actual physical re-selection below a strict IoU selector and malformed escape must stay in the outcome.

The later successor-scale A/B pair (catalog:2026-09-13-owner-successor-scale-throughput) reduced repetition but lost confirmation coverage. A negative term's practical value must be measured jointly with positive realization, later consequences, old-owner retention and stopping, not by negative-event loss alone.

## Reopening condition

Choose the actual preservation obligation before choosing its surrogate. Compare state-specific alternatives and actual owner transitions, and distinguish source-reference exposure from independent confirmation. Keep optimizer exposure, number of replays and wall time explicit when comparing CE, RLOO and constrained updates; matching one does not match the others.

The completed 22-image cumulative fit (catalog:2026-09-15-coco22-cumulative-expansion) allowed intermediate regression but required final stage completion. That contract does not impose perfect preservation at every SGD step on future studies. Conversely, aggregate gains must not erase final identity losses. For the proposed recurrence-row contrast (catalog:2026-09-17-readout-norm-fresh128), track subsequent free gains and losses, including credible unlabeled incumbents; the supplied owner itself gets no recovery credit. Uncertain losses remain visible, rather than disappearing when review cannot confidently assign identity. [Research entry](../index.md) owns the current proposal boundary.

## Retained specialization boundary

The former standalone specialization decision is integrated here: improving row designation or a training surrogate does not establish better natural enumeration. Keep before/after coverage, duplication, validity, closure and retained native capabilities alongside the intended gain. The underlying PVCI proposal-bridge evidence (catalog:2026-07-11-pvci-causal-proposal-bridge) remains scoped to its original setup. This synthesis does not grant compute or mandate a new experiment.

## Current reopening boundary

Reopen when a dose-matched comparison can test a particular preservation mechanism without changing the reviewed owner universe. Intermediate optimization may trade off terms; the final gate must state its allowed losses and debt. Do not let unknown loss disappear from the denominator.
