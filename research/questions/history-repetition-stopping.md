# History, repetition and stopping

## Question and evidence unit

Why can an autoregressive detector enter a long run of the same or nearby rows,
and what distinguishes changing its output from fixing the relevant cause?
Keep separate: token margin/choice, complete-row conditional likelihood, free
complete-row realization, sustained trajectory behavior and physical-owner
coverage. Numerical return is not physical revisit; a cap is not natural EOS.
"Basin" remains a metaphor, not a demonstrated stationary attractor in growing
position/KV state. Most late-September contrasts use mature untied+axis001
step-2444; earlier Source/norm contrasts also use tied models. This is not a
controlled untie-only comparison. See [model/data identities](../assets.md).
Supplied histories and object headers are not ordinary empty-history discovery.

## Current interpretation

No single readout norm, coordinate-validity rule, tied embedding, fixed-prior-row
copying mechanism or portable positional clock explains all observed bursts.
History and image/context information affect choices, but local actuation alone
does not establish a coverage ledger, physical-recall gain or training origin.
The working hypothesis is conditional route/coverage access with competing
history-conditioned evidence; this is a hypothesis, not an identified disease.

## Stop and reopen conditions

Do not reopen closed layer/head/donor/extent/horizon searches solely because they
alter logits. Reopen with independently supported physical semantics, a prediction
separating plausible explanations and a declared full-native outcome at the right
evidence level. A changed adjudication of the unique pilot stripe would require
re-reading the necessary clause, not retroactively calling today's NONPASS a pass.
