# History, repetition and stopping: what state does a row leave behind?

**Question:** do output changes follow image evidence, supplied token history, checkpoint parameters, or their interaction? A short non-repeating prefix is not proof of escape from later recurrence.

## Earlier commit and binding lessons

The [June precursor synthesis](../experiments/2026-06-10_12_pre_onset_duplication_precursors/results.md) found selected state signals before the duplicate was emitted; this was an early-warning hypothesis, not a validated general predictor. The [x1/y1 case studies](../experiments/2026-06-26_27_case_studies_and_x1_y1_routes/results.md) showed sharp train/validation and sample differences. Coordinate readability, owner binding and useful continuation must remain separate.

The [native write-read bridge](../experiments/2026-07-11-pvci-endogenous-commit-write-read-bridge/unit.md) admitted only 10 of 15 source continuations with a valid attributable uncovered-object row. Their generated coordinates could write a one-step spatial suppression signal, including nearby zero-token-overlap geometry. The five unidentifiable rows did not become successful writes. The [source-swap study](../experiments/2026-07-11-pvci-commit-field-source-swap/unit.md) rejected indiscriminate suppression at arbitrary written coordinates, but real annotated support alone was insufficient.

The resulting current-selection-gate hypothesis was itself narrowed by [selection-aligned prefix-state gating](../experiments/2026-07-11-pvci-selection-aligned-prefix-state-gating/unit.md): generic history/depth/trajectory phase fit that panel better than target-specific selection. [Identity-by-slot counterbalancing](../experiments/2026-07-11-pvci-native-identity-slot-counterbalance/unit.md) and [canonical-depth continuation](../experiments/2026-07-11-pvci-native-canonical-commit-depth/unit.md) supported order-conditioned transitions, not an order-free persistent ledger. These are tested predecessors, not a queue of unexecuted July plans.

## Evidence chain

[Phrase/geometry factorials](../experiments/2026-07-15-prefix-state-phrase-geometry-factorial/results.md), [native sibling-row crossings](../experiments/2026-07-17-native-sibling-row-branch-value-and-commit-crossover/results.md) and [same-covered-set permutations](../experiments/2026-07-19-common-object-prefix-permutation-short-horizon/results.md) established bounded row/history effects without an order-independent covered-owner ledger.
