# Ideas that should not disappear

This is a selective decision-oriented register, not an exhaustive substitute for the [full experiment catalog](experiments.jsonl) or frozen source corpus. Search both before declaring an idea new. “Not tested” and “technically unanswered” are not negative results. A closed recipe is not a permanent ban on its whole family. None of these entries authorizes execution.

## Before proposing a new unit

Write one short predecessor note: nearest experiment and exact tested conditions; what it actually answered; which idea remains unanswered; what will differ; what possible observation changes the decision. Use the catalog's title/topic and source paths, then read the closest result and protocol. If no suitable predecessor exists, say so. Do not create a ceremonial review chain or a new experiment just to fill a table.
