# Research: trustworthy natural set completion

Reduce physical false negatives on incompletely labelled images through
original-image, empty-prefix natural greedy enumeration. More sampled owners,
better conditional scores or fewer invalid boxes are not automatically better
free physical-owner coverage.

## Interpretation and execution boundaries

Unknown/unmatched is not a negative label. Keep owner identity, visibility,
category, geometry and part/group granularity separate. Preserve original
annotations, frozen evaluation identities and the distinction between conditional
and wholly free outputs. A technically invalid contrast remains unanswered.

Use the [glossary](glossary.md), [literature map](literature/index.md) and
[alternatives](alternatives.md) when they can change the interpretation or next
discriminator. Old grants, historical running states and proposed next steps do
not authorize new work. The [convention](CONVENTIONS.md) owns research knowledge;
[probe infrastructure](../docs/RESEARCH_PROBE_INFRA_BASE.md) owns reusable
mechanics and [storage policy](../docs/OUTPUT_STORAGE_POLICY.md) distinguishes
maintained source, raw evidence and current-run captures.
