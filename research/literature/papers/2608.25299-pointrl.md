# PointRL

## Source and reading scope

Su et al., **PointRL: Learning Point-Level Vision-Language Grounding from Verifiable Annotation Evidence**, [arXiv:2608.25299v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25299v1), 2026-08-26. Checked 2026-09-17: abstract and selected task/data/verifier sections, especially §§III–IV. No local replication or full reward-code audit.

## Tested conditions and observations

The model receives an image and instruction; annotations supply a hidden verifier target set and constraints. Verification ignores point order but retains repeated predictions. A valid multi-target response must satisfy support membership, distinct-target coverage, cardinality and other instruction constraints. The training-data construction admits rule-bound target sets; reported same-backbone evaluations show improved pointing performance.

## Authors' interpretation and our assessment

The paper proposes verifiable set-level feedback for the non-unique space of acceptable points. We can borrow the separation between an instance's identity, its valid geometric support and the collection-level outcome. This is evidence for a recipe under its verifier, not proof that each reward component is universally necessary or that the model internally represents an explicit instance ledger.

## Limits and alternatives

A deterministic checker can be reproducible while its annotation-defined targets remain incomplete relative to physical reality. On our incompletely labeled images, treating every unmatched prediction or excess count as wrong could penalize real objects. Point validity also does not establish accurate box extent. Joint recipe improvement alone cannot isolate the causal contribution of each reward term.
