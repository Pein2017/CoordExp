# Using OCR Heads to Verbalize Image Semantics

## Source, version and reading scope

Feucht et al., [arXiv 2609.18823v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18823v1), 2026-09-16. Checked 2026-09-17: selected [sections 2–4 and Appendices C/F/I](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18823v1), including localization denominators and editing scope. No local replication.

## Tested setting and observations

Across four VLMs, mean-ablation of OCR-ranked heads damages single-token transcription more than matched random late-layer ablation. Combining their OV transformations with unembedding exposes image semantics from layer 0. In Qwen3-VL-8B, 29/100 gaze heads overlap with verbalization heads. Localization scores use category-union masks, not separate same-category instances: all people share one target mask. Qwen3-VL-8B token mIoU is 0.190 versus raw-lens 0.161. Concept editing uses a scaled, truncated-pseudoinverse direction across image tokens and layers.

## Authors' interpretation versus our assessment

The authors interpret OCR heads as broader verbalization components. Our assessment: the lens is a useful diagnostic of accessible semantic content. Transformed layer-0 decodability does not establish correct native consumption, owner-specific binding or accurate box generation. Partial head overlap also does not justify treating gaze, retrieval and verbalization as interchangeable circuit labels.

## Limits and strongest alternative

Our strongest alternative is that concept-level steering changes which category gets verbalized without selecting a particular physical owner. Category-union localization cannot distinguish two neighboring people, and a successful caption edit cannot certify instance-preserving geometry. An externally pointed token is a supplied selector; it is not evidence of autonomous discovery. Separate semantic availability, selector provenance and full-row realization before assigning a mechanism.

## Local questions, predecessors and rereading trigger

Read with [capacity and readout](../../questions/capacity-and-readout.md) and [visual designation and causal use](../../questions/visual-designation-and-causal-use.md). The latter's held-out representation gate and failed own-prefix behavioral predecessor already separate readable proposal information from useful delivery.

Reread when a local diagnostic needs an alternative semantic readout or a category-specific intervention. Keep raw-lens and matched-head controls; require owner-discriminating evidence for an instance claim. Use the paper to sharpen the measurement boundary before adding another lens. Do not infer that high category confidence resolves the successor-selection problem or supersedes an existing full-trajectory counterexample.
