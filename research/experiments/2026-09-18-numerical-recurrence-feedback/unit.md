## Evidence and alternatives

The mature comparison and active-readout units establish that tying is unnecessary
for observed repetition, and that output norms affect decisions but do not explain
all endpoint wins. Old common-tail tests found whole-row and first-coordinate
effects of opposite sign; they did not isolate immediate recent-coordinate
feedback. One-bin rescues and cache patches establish control, not selective
maintenance. The gradient unit remains technical-invalid and is not a prerequisite.

The new production-task val200 summary reports, for untied+axis, RP1, cap3084,
normalization mAP0.4631532183 ->0.4803275126 and FN623 ->608. RP1.10 mAP decreases
slightly; val885/5586 still loop. This is corroborating, separately conditioned
evidence, not a universally beneficial policy or an untie-only causal estimate.
Source: `/data/CoordExp/outputs/infra_base/tie-untie-comparison-20260918/summary.json`.

Strong alternatives are persistent image/context preference, ordinary spatial
progression, current-row legality, and generic sensitivity to unlikely histories.
A positive historical response shared by healthy states can still contribute to
failure; it does not identify a failure-specific module. Fixed-suffix persistence
can be direct rereading of the original changed row, rather than output relay.
