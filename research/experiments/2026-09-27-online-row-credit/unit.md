## First complete recipe

L_image = M + 0.25 R + redirect + legal. These are starting coefficients, not calibrated gradient equality. Persist each component, counts and selected-position derivatives, and measure natural behavior. Empty components are differentiable zero; no negative signed CE.

M rewards fresh valid retained-matched first occurrences at their actual generated prefixes. Match retained references against the first occurrence of each literal-valid key using class-agnostic one-to-one IoU.5 matching then category agreement; do not lose all positive credit merely because matching selected a later identical repeat. This training population is declared separately from full-reference raw-output evaluation. A repeated literal row never receives competing M credit. Use actual output tokens with CE+.1type_gate+.01conditional_order_gate, row mean within image, local opener/closure included and terminal EOS excluded. Unmatched rows are unknown, not negative labels.

R is the existing coherent retained-label row/image objective over all513 labels, weighted0.25. It preserves supervision when an incumbent drops out of the new rollout. It is explicitly rendered teacher forcing, distinct from actual generated prefixes, with the same complete-row atoms and terminal/EOS censoring. This supplies available-label learning, not a claim that omitted labels are neutral.

Redirect selects the first eligible complete exact duplicate in generation order, at most one per image/round; exact identity is description plus canonical coordinate bins, including certified complete-invalid rows. Keep every observation. Let h be literal tokens before the duplicate opener and y- the actual repeated row. Select a positive retained-supported alternative not previously represented: no preceding valid same-category row may overlap its retained box at IoU>=.5, and its proposed row cannot literally equal any preceding complete row. Prefer the earliest later first-occurrence row with a retained category-agreeing match satisfying these conditions; otherwise render the first eligible retained annotation in canonical(x1,y1,annotation_id) order. The latter is explicit known-FN repair. Record provenance/eligibility and annotation identity; no physical-owner equivalence claim. If none is eligible, keep the event as unsupported and assign no redirect term; never invent EOS or hidden targets.

At the first differing token d of y+ and y-, their earlier row tokens must be identical. Require a semantic description/coordinate difference rather than schema mismatch. The negative term is softplus(1 + z(y-_d) - z(y+_d)) at this one shared-prefix causal decision; compute both logits from the positive replay's same position. Add the ordinary positive complete-row CE+.1type+.01order loss for y+ appended to h. Do not suppress shared row-opening/closing syntax, multiply row probabilities, length-normalize an unlikelihood, or supervise a successor suffix/EOS. Preserve all original M prefixes without silently rebasing them after the correction. Positive alternatives reduce the incentive for arbitrary jitter but do not prove its absence.

Legal penalizes invalid/type-escaping coordinate choices on every certified complete-row coordinate decision of the fresh trace, including currently legal boxes. At actual target position t, use logit t-1 and loss logsumexp(all vocabulary logits) - logsumexp(legal coordinate-token logits). x1/y1 allow0..998; x2/y2 allow bins strictly above that row's actual preceding x1/y1. When a start is999, supervise the offending start and omit its impossible empty-set end decision. Never repair/round/clamp generated coordinates into GT constructors. Reversal/equality/end-invalid slots remain legal-set losses, not CE toward an invented correct coordinate. This is a training loss only. Use slot mean within each complete row, then row mean within each image; repeated occurrences are real different prefixes, not confidence votes. Malformed/incomplete spans without certified coordinate grammar remain unscored burdens, never silently dropped from evaluation.

No generic FN-at-EOS or EOS-replacement reward in this first recipe. Unknown rows keep indirect prefix influence but no standalone confidence reward. No IoU-near-duplicate negative labels. Subsequent ablations require lead decisions based on the first live evidence.
