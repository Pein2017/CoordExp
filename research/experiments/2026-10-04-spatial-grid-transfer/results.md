## Decision and inference

Recognizable conditional bottle localization occurs beyond the18-image
optimization cohort, but the useful resolved entities are already in this case's
baseline. The result supports neither positive incremental recovery on25394 nor
a general failure to localize bottles on other images. It does not identify
memorization: image, native history and available recovery opportunity changed
together. Validation/model-selection and base-pretraining exposure remain unknown.
This is not a wholly unseen benchmark or a population transfer estimate.

The concrete query computation revisits one early bottle state with a supplied
x1 and completes the remaining coordinates. It receives no list of entities
found later in the baseline, so repeating later baseline discoveries is compatible
with the computation. This behavioral observation does not identify an internal
attention route or prove causal visual reliance on the transfer image.

Root stops expansion of this fixed-grid branch after the finite contrast and
reference check. The prior nine-identity training-image result remains valid
within its scope; this negative case narrows the benefit claim. Do not search
for a favorable image, densify the grid, tune a confidence threshold or convert
these outputs into training labels under this consumed release. Ordinary-policy
entry and reliable candidate validation remain separate unresolved questions.
