## Interpretation and limits

The surviving account is strong conditional route dependence: earlier relative placement affects decisions; full-time versus late norm intervention differs; changed token decisions can return to the same or another loop. These observations do not identify a separate circuit, coordinate-embedding defect, sorted-training necessity, or isolated count feedback. A successful sampled pulse is conditional continuation evidence, not empty-prefix greedy improvement. Unknown predictions are not FP, and changed GT matching is not physical disappearance.

Any further work needs a new decision-changing contrast: a matched replay of a successful versus failed sampled history with coherent row-level changes could separate spatial/format route change from physical-owner novelty. This package does not execute it or tune pulse doses.
