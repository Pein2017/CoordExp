# VF6 mechanics results — 2026-07-11

## Scope and verdict

The bounded real two-step A/B/C smoke and the same-checkpoint C-on/C-off
own-prefix parity panel completed successfully.  This is **mechanics-only
evidence**.  It does not establish proposal learnability, recall improvement,
or benchmark superiority.

Current verdicts:

- mechanics VF6: `PASS`;

  teacher-forced vector producer, complete frozen gate decision, and accepted

## Remaining launch gates

Before the 512-step screen can be authorized:

1. the strict teacher-forced proposal-vector producer must load adapter-only
   A/B/C checkpoints and canonical cached packed inputs directly, then pass a
   real Qwen/FA2 artifact smoke;
2. all frozen representation metrics and controls must be present before
   training evidence is interpreted, including target rank, footprint-union
   localization, center distance, overlap-adjusted specificity, coverage
   masses, matched controls, image-first aggregation, and paired bootstrap;
3. the evaluator must emit the frozen aggregate gate decision and fail closed
   on absent controls rather than manufacture or relabel them;
4. the accepted own-prefix evaluation producer must compose complete
   checkpoints for A, B, C-off, and C-on beyond the two fixed VF6 requests;
5. pulse-cap activation must remain first-class monitoring.

No full architecture claim is made here.  The evidence establishes the
smallest needed fact: the proposal objective and the causal pulse can be wired,
trained, saved, reloaded, disabled exactly, and made to affect Qwen3-VL logits
through the declared path without contaminating other arms.
