## Decision and frozen question

From unchanged Stable50 and the exact original-image native early pre-repeat
prefix `h`, does supplying one visually admitted, geometry-valid complete row
`c` for a different plausible object permit a finite and useful newly generated
suffix, compared with both a fresh natural anchor and free continuation from
`h` alone?

This unit acquires repair-witness candidates for a later negative-only versus
positive-plus-negative learning decision. It is not training, evidence of a
learned policy, or permission to change an architecture, loss, dose, cohort,
checkpoint, or held-out axis. Root owns visual admission, launch, scientific
interpretation, and final acceptance.

## Contrast and accounting

For each frozen case, preserve these cells only:

1. `natural_anchor`: a fresh unforced natural rollout from the original image,
   total action cap 3,084; it must exactly reproduce the prior Stable50 action
   tokens before conditional cells for that case are accepted.
2. `h_only`: a free continuation from exact original-image native `h`, with
   `3,084 - len(h)` generated-token allowance.
3. Up to two `h_plus_c` cells: supply exactly one admitted complete candidate
   row `c` after `h`, then generate a fresh suffix with
   `3,084 - len(h) - len(c)` allowance.

There are at most 16 continuation cells: four natural, four `h_only`, and at
most eight candidate cells. A smoke cell is reused in the final panel and is
not regenerated. Unused candidate slots are not reassigned.

The forced `c` is recorded separately from the fresh suffix. It is never
included in native owner recovery, useful free-row counts, or EOS evidence.
Native EOS is a stop observation, not a positive outcome. Complete invalid
free rows and the incomplete terminal fragment, if any, stay visible in raw
parser burden rather than being silently dropped. No later row is forced, no
row-rejection search is allowed, and every suffix is generated afresh.

The primary observation is per candidate: native EOS versus length censoring;
complete valid and complete invalid **free** rows; literal free-token sequence;
and root's original-image visual judgment of whether the free continuation is
useful while preserving plausible prior objects. Natural anchor parity and
`h_only` are controls, not positive demonstrations. Because `c` is forced,
this unit can establish conditional realization after a supplied row, not
natural selection of that row or trainability.

## Strongest alternative and stop rule

The strongest alternative is that any finite suffix is merely an off-policy
reaction to a forced row, including premature EOS or omission of previously
plausible objects, rather than evidence that the row provides useful positive
credit. The exact `h_only` control, fresh natural parity anchor, separate forced
row accounting, raw invalid burden, and root visual review are the cheapest
discriminators available within this acquisition.

Stop when visual admission is frozen and every admitted cell is either durably
acquired and cold-read, or its technical failure is bounded. No training,
dose expansion, new candidate source, forced repair chain, architecture change,
or extra cohort case follows from this unit. A technical-invalid case remains
scientifically unanswered; a `HOLD` candidate is not a negative.
