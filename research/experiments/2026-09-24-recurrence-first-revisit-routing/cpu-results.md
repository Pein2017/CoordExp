The five-image CPU preparation is lead-accepted within the distinctions below.
The candidate report and JSON remain byte-unchanged. Root independently checked
20 raw/trace/receipt/image bindings, 22 retained native row slices and their
chosen-token traces, and all nine common-prefix/fork crosswalks.

cross-image controls, not a causal matched cohort. The chair is an invalid
multi-owner proposal recurrence and remains separate.

rank33. They must not be used to place that prospective image at global index33
ahead of mature images: the population ordering is mature145 then prospective128.
No new source-order selection is performed here.

## Why the proposed score-only replay is not admitted

For the proposed native pairs, source chosen IDs equal raw global winners at
the common x1 fork, and the other candidate is not even the runner-up. Thus the
finite pair's winner direction at that fork is already established. Bowl row1
favors A; bowl row2, cow row1 and glass row4 favor their native new candidate.
Unknown exact margins and complete-row scores do not by themselves explain
why the preference formed. Previous finite row-choice screens already bound
the greedy-versus-complete-row alternative. Twelve more calls solely to recover
those scores are not the chosen next experiment.

Instead the [native-read protocol](native-history-read-protocol.md) asks for
the sign of direct historical-read influence at the original first fork.
It prioritizes bowl row1 versus row2 with the same fixed A/B extents in the
same image; cow row1 is a separate normal-transition reference. This remains
a local influence test, not a physical-recovery or population experiment.

All preparation and lead checks are CPU-only: zero model/vision/GPU calls.
The original scientific proposal was not accepted merely because its source
arithmetic was valid. See [acceptance](lead-cpu-acceptance.json).
