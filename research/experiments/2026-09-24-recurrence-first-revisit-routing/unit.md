## Computational account under consideration

For one attention head, historical selection depends on
`q_t^T R(p_j-p_t) k_j / sqrt(d)`, its softmax competition with all other visible
keys, and the values returned through the output projection and later layers.
Here q and k denote pre-rotary vectors; the formula is per rotary component
under the model's actual layout. A large response to changing a key's phase
does not establish that the native key is faulty, or that keys dominate image,
query, value, residual or readout contributions.

At the tested nine-token row layout, current x1 to latest historical y1 has
native relative distance 8. K+9 makes it -1 while preserving physical causal
order. Native row advancement preserves distance 8. Thus an artificial address
collision can expose a coordinate-content pathway without being a natural
onset explanation. A native onset hypothesis needs a change in contextual
query/key/value state, competition with older/image/prompt sources, or readout;
it cannot simply invoke that artificial +9 movement.

Working hypothesis: current-row coordinates progressively restrict the
supported object/region; recently emitted geometry can bias that restriction.
Failure may begin before the first discriminating coordinate, or only after
a prefix is written. A visitedness-dependent preference is not guaranteed by
accurate object localization. Strong alternatives are a coherent native
preference for the old region from the outset, visual ambiguity, and ordinary
coordinate serialization shared by successful transitions. These may coexist.
