## Interpretation limitation accepted from scientific review

The frozen reducer's `pure_tail` field means an owner is realized outside the
union of direct prefix/action compatible-edge owner sets. It is **not** proof
that the suffix changed causally: a fixed suffix prediction compatible with B/C
can be reassigned from B to C when a changed action acquires B. C is outside the
direct action-incidence set. The primary owner sets and contrasts stay correct.

Root accepts this counterexample and will not use incidence-filtered counts as
unique causal suffix attribution. The strongest211674 witness is unaffected:
both actions have zero compatible GT edges at all three thresholds, so adding
either cannot alter the assignment graph among suffix predictions. No frozen
primary metric, input, selection or shared scientific packet is changed.
