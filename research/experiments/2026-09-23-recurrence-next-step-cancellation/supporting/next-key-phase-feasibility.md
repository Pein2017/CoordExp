As a deliberately conservative planning estimate, give **no credit** for this case's smaller batch/width, scale that elapsed by `11/5`, then apply 2× margin: `178.271` seconds = `0.049520` allocated GPU-hours. Added to the accepted cumulative `0.069672503` hours gives `0.119192122` hours, below the retained 8-hour ceiling and a possible 0.25-hour incremental ceiling. This is a planning estimate, not a runtime guarantee or admission. An implementation should hard-stop against whatever finite cap the lead freezes and count setup/failures. Retaining only eleven full vectors, two nine-token A K/V/pre-K/phase blocks across28 layers, hashes and bounded consumer records should keep raw output well below the older 183.8 MB tracing payload; propose a 64 MiB artifact envelope and measure it. Do not serialize the entire historical cache.
