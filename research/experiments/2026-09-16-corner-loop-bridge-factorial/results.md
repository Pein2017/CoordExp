## What the fixed factorial establishes

- C10 changes only chair→person; the supplied box stays zero-height and all four coordinate tokens are unchanged. Therefore restoring supplied-box validity is not necessary for this observed escape/recovery.
- C01 changes only y1:999→0; category stays chair. Therefore switching to person is not necessary either. This geometry edit changes validity, area and extent together, so its mechanism is not isolated.
- C11 adds no known-owner benefit over either single edit. One fixed history cannot establish a general interaction law or independence of mechanisms.
- All arms retain the same coordinate embeddings/readout and language DoRA checkpoint. The effect comes from the supplied recent history. This does not rule out an embedding-origin contribution to the underlying tendency.
- Recurrence at all999 shows that x1=0 is not required for the later pathology. It does not identify a KV circuit, copy algorithm or global endpoint prior.

Strongest surviving explanation: exact recent trajectory content can change the local continuation route enough to access useful enumeration, while a broader tendency toward degenerate endpoint repetition remains. The useful effect could reflect semantic/geometry cues or generic disruption of a repeatedly copied row; this factorial does not identify their common internal mechanism.
