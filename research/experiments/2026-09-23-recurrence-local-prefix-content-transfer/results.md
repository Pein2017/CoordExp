## Interpretation and stop

The current row's contextual prefix can tip a native first difference, as shown
by351017. It is insufficient to restore the other2 selected exact-row repeats
under this fixed donor/intervention. All27 upper layers actually changed prefix
K/V in every image, so these are not unexecuted replacements. These observations
weaken the preferred explanation that this local prefix is the dominant common
carrier across the three images. The retained current-query state and older
history remain viable alternatives; this assay does not separate those two.

The result is not a population estimate, count-only mechanism, unique circuit,
natural onset/exit-time predictor, or physical recovery. Donor contextual content
already includes earlier positional effects; holding destination RoPE fixed does
not make it purely semantic. Exact-token exits may continue owner recurrence or
later cap. No new generation, training or owner-level benefit was measured.

Close this branch without donor, layer, head, strength or extra-image rescue
scans. The useful remaining question is prospective prediction of natural
transitions from the native trajectory, distinct from another sensitivity scan;
no successor/model job is launched by this closeout.
