## What this branch establishes and leaves open

Across the preceding experiments, recent-row key phase carries conditional output-distribution changes across both exact-repeat and different-coordinate histories. In informative D cases, the artificial phase displacement increases prior-y1 versus current-y1 preference. The present intervention links part of that response to one historical y1 value, with a clear limiting case. Together these are a concrete conditional computational account, not evidence that the same displacement occurs naturally during duplication bursts.

This branch is closed without another slot, donor, head, layer or threshold scan. Natural first-arrival/revisit onset and persistence remain unanswered. Reopening should require a distinct native-trajectory prediction that discriminates this account from ordinary recent-context use, rather than another fixed-state sensitivity or repair of the failed shared bound.
