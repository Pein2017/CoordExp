## Evidence and physical criteria

- Each view_image call must display ONE sample at sufficient resolution,
  preferably detail=original. No multi-sample contact sheets as evidence.
  Prefer a native-size full annotated frame; separately enlarge small or
  ambiguous same-sample targets. Do not squeeze many samples into thumbnails.
- The index binds exact c and immediate w, the raw image and exact history h.
  Inspect c and w separately for entity, COCO-80 class, plausible full VISIBLE
  extent, and physical newness. w must be distinct from c and prior h owners.
  Low IoU does not prove newness; different boxes can depict the same instance.
- Occlusion/image truncation is allowed when the visible instance is coherent.
  Do not invent an amodal box or demand pixel-perfect boundaries. Conversely,
  a substantial missing part or a union of separate owners is not a trusted
  full row just because an object exists or IoU would exceed0.5.
- Dense books/fruit/vegetables/cups can be valid group coverage under user
  annotation policy, but are HOLD for this frozen SINGLETON training bank.
  They are not hallucinations or negative labels. No generic fruit/vegetable
  class, forced DVD->book, rabbit toy->teddy bear, or bouquet->potted plant.
- GT-unmatched is neutral. First repeated owner truth is a separate axis;
  duplication does not establish hallucination or c/w trust.
- Existing exact visual groups may share a viewed scene, but each job's exact
  h/newness must be checked separately. Do not blindly propagate a group label
  to different histories. Re-view context if needed. Preserve physical aliases
  as evidence; different histories are not additional physical-owner gains.

Observed counterexamples: a bench w omitted its left third; a suitcase w
omitted a substantial left section/lid; flowers in a vase were called a certain
potted plant. All depicted real entities, but did not establish full row trust.
Read `U/physical-admission/root-individual-review-v1.json` only if that distinction
is unclear; those examples are not additional review samples.
