## Numerical-address evidence

The fixed48-forward assay passed eight exact full-vocabulary identity replays. Independent softmax reconstruction from saved1000-coordinate logits reproduces the conditional means within9.17e-8. Early x1 same-axis plus-minus response is positive in1/4 cases, early y1 in4/4; late x1 in4/4, late y1 in1/4. Wrong-axis responses can be comparable or larger. Same-axis mean changes range approximately-0.35 to+0.98 coordinate bins, despite the supplied address field moving by up to100 bins between opposite warps. Those input/output scales are descriptive, not an expected unit-gain law or evidence of universal insensitivity.

These four fixed-prefix cases do not establish coherent two-axis numerical transport. In particular, sensitivity to earlier tuple corruption was not sufficient evidence that the model read an explicit coordinate ruler. A possible remaining explanation is that projected output embeddings behave as another position-correlated feature, while ordinary SFT provides no explicit requirement to invert them into numeric addresses. That is a hypothesis, not an identified internal computation: small assay support, teacher-forced history and competing native position information leave alternatives. The experiment does not locate information loss in vision, merger or LLM layers, nor establish a duplication-burst mechanism.
