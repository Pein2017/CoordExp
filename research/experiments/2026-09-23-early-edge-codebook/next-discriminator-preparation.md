## Cost and scope

Accepted early eight-rank fitting took 1,970.42 wall-seconds and 15,763.38 allocated GPU-seconds; its 1,280-cell eight-worker final evaluation took 12,978.76 allocated GPU-seconds, with slowest worker 1,800.47 seconds. The early root, including a failed four-rank attempt and other stages, used 31,886.81 GPU-seconds over 5,705.48 wall-seconds. Its fit directory was about 2.2 GiB and final cells about 184 MiB. These measurements suggest roughly 8 GPU-hours for matched fit plus full decode before qualification/assay/failures; long capped outputs, loader differences and repair time limit extrapolation. Propose a **new, lead-frozen ceiling of 4 model wall-hours, 32 allocated GPU-hours and 8 GiB**, counting failures, loading and in-flight work. Stop new model admission at 3.75 hours and reserve 15 minutes to persist/join. Final off1280 cells take priority over the 48-forward diagnostic; unfinished planned cells remain HOLD. This is a proposal, not inherited authorization. A smaller 32-image saturation fit cannot answer the 1024-panel utility question at matched data and dose.

Only future scoped probe changes would be an off first-entry wrapper/category handling, a four-condition reducer and its focused caller test, and a one-purpose assay runner/test.
