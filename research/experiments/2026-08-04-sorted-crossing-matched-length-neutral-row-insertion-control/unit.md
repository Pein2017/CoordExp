# Sorted Crossing Matched-Length Neutral-Row Insertion Control

## Decision and outcome

The completed [crossing-boundary owner release and realization
unit](../2026-08-03-sorted-crossing-boundary-owner-release-realization/results.md)
found a heterogeneous downstream exact-row likelihood tail after inserting the
clean skipped-owner row. The completed [geometric-relation
stratification](../2026-08-04-sorted-crossing-owner-row-geometric-relation-stratification/results.md)
showed that overlap or duplicate suppression cannot explain the whole tail:
four of nine material coordinate changes are clearly separated. Generic
added-row, position, or recency sensitivity nevertheless remains joined with a
content-specific interaction.

This unit asks one bounded question:

> At the frozen sorted crossing boundaries where inserting the exact clean row
> for skipped owner `C` materially lowered the likelihood of the exact
> downstream row `E` coordinate tokens, does inserting a real, already-covered,
> different-description, geometrically separated, length-matched control row
> reproduce that materiality?

The control row is called the **neutral row** and is denoted `N`. "Neutral"
means neutral only with respect to the declared description and geometry
relations. It is not fully neutral: because it was already emitted before the
boundary, inserting it is both a duplicate and a regression in the learned
sorted route. Those unavoidable confounds make the interpretation asymmetric.
Nonmaterial `N` is a stacked-deck test supporting a `C`-content-specific
reading; material `N` can show that `C`-specific content is not required, but
cannot prove a pure generic-insertion mechanism.

The completed unit may update only the mechanism ranking at these frozen boundaries. It
cannot promote a training treatment, inference policy, owner head, slot,
coverage ledger, architecture, or final-set claim.

## Exact state and row terminology

For each target physical owner:

- `C` is the supported owner that the native geometry-sorted trajectory skips;
- `P` is the exact native self-prefix at the last root/ahead boundary before
  the next native row moves `C` behind the sorted frontier;
- `E` is that exact next native emitted row, including its realized description
  and coordinates; and
- `N` is the deterministic neutral control row defined below and inserted at
  the same `P` boundary where the clean row for `C` is inserted.

The primary score always belongs to the same four sealed coordinate tokens of
`E`. Only the conditioning prefix changes.

## Deterministic neutral-row selection

Selection is a CPU-only pure function of the sealed owner, category,
candidate-bank, native-sidecar, crossing-cohort, and geometry registries. It
may not inspect new model output, historical per-owner likelihood deltas beyond
the frozen material/nonmaterial cohort label, or manual preferences.

A candidate `N` must satisfy all conditions:

1. it is a same-image census owner, is not excluded from the census, and is
   neither `C` nor the strict-matched physical owner of `E`;
2. it strict-matched a native emitted row at row index less than crossing
   boundary `P`, so it was covered before `P`;
3. its normalized description differs from both `C` and the realized
   description of `E`;
4. its norm-1000 box has intersection-over-union (IoU) `0` with both `C` and
   `E`, and neither box center
   is contained by the other in either pair;
5. its full clean row token length differs from the clean `C` row by at most
   two tokens.

Candidates are ordered by:

1. smallest absolute row-token-length difference from `C`;
2. latest strict-matched native row index before `P`, minimizing sorted-route
   regression;
3. largest minimum normalized center distance to `C` and `E`; and
4. ascending physical-owner ID.

The first candidate is selected. Record the row-length delta, matched native
row index, rows-back distance, sorted-route regression, both center distances,
minimum center distance, geometry relations, candidate count, and whether only
one candidate existed. No minimum center-distance floor, alternate neutral row,
uncovered-row arm, position-gap arm, sampling, or manual reselection is part of
this unit.

`N` is assembled without retokenization from the sealed category query suffix,
the owner's sealed `exact_gt_anchor` four coordinate tokens, and the exact
`<|box_end|>` token. The inserted `C` row and scored `E` row use their sealed
predecessor token identities.

## Requests and score semantics

Execute `21` owners: the nine voting owners and twelve specificity owners.
Every paired request scores a baseline root and a modified root in the same
runtime. The score-only capture includes:

1. `21` `P` versus `P+N` pairs, scoring the exact sealed `E` row;
2. `21` `P` versus `P+C` replay pairs, scoring the same exact sealed `E` row;
3. all `12` frozen benign-substitution pairs, replayed in the same runtime and
   scoring their sealed following native row.

This is approximately `54` paired requests. Description, coordinate, and
complete-row sums are recorded separately; the primary estimand uses only the
four exact coordinate tokens.

For owner `i`, define:

```text
neutral_coordinate_delta(i)
  = logprob(E coordinates | P+N) - logprob(E coordinates | P)

relative_neutral_coordinate_delta(i)
  = neutral_coordinate_delta(i)
    - same_image_benign_coordinate_delta(image(i))
```

The existing image-referenced cutoff is unchanged: `N` is material iff
`relative_neutral_coordinate_delta <= -1.0` nat.
`same_image_benign_coordinate_delta` is the same-run benign replay value; the
sealed benign delta is a gate reference only and never enters the estimand. The
same-run `C` relative delta uses the same same-run benign reference, and its raw
delta and materiality are checked against the sealed predecessor values.
Same-image rows share one benign reference but remain owner-level votes; raw
likelihoods are never pooled across images.

No free decode, greedy coordinate decode, sampling, rollout, or image `2299`
execution is authorized.

## Validity gates

Evaluate gates in this fixed order before any scientific route:

1. **Input and token identity.** Bind all source digests. For each owner, the
   scored `E` token IDs and digest must be identical between the `C` and `N`
   arms. Inserted row grammar and candidate identity must reconstruct exactly.
2. **Runtime replay.** Every new-run `P -> E` coordinate baseline must preserve
   the compared argmax and agree with the sealed selected-logit sum within
   `1e-3`. A mismatch quarantines that owner; more than two quarantined owners
   stops the unit for runtime repair.
3. **Same-run positive controls.** Every executed `P+C -> E` replay must
   reproduce the frozen sign and materiality. Its raw coordinate delta must
   agree with the sealed value within `0.05` nat. More than two failures stops
   the unit. The named sentinel owners `gt:13923:14` and `gt:4134:27` must both
   pass regardless of the total count.
4. **Benign-reference replay.** All twelve benign pairs must reproduce their
   sealed sign and materiality, with each selected-token sum preserving the
   compared argmax and agreeing with the sealed sum within `1e-3`, and each
   benign coordinate delta agreeing with the sealed delta within `0.05` nat.
   Any mismatch is visible and blocks image-referenced interpretation for
   affected images.
5. **Cached-versus-uncached parity.** A representative matched-`E`,
   unmatched-`E`, same-description, and different-description smoke must have
   maximum selected-logit difference at most `1e-3` and preserve every compared
   argmax and materiality sign. Otherwise use the predecessor's uncached
   fallback and quarantine semantics.
6. **Neutral-row specificity.** If `N` is material for at least four of the
   frozen twelve specificity owners, route
   `neutral_row_not_neutral_inconclusive`; do not interpret material `N` as
   generic-insertion evidence for the voting owners. A quarantined owner never
   counts as material, so the threshold remains the absolute integer four even
   when fewer than twelve owners are interpretable.

## Frozen routes

After the validity and specificity gates, apply exactly one route in order.

### `c_content_specific_interference_survives`

Route when `N` is nonmaterial for at least `5/9` voting owners across at least
three images, including at least `2/4` clearly separated owners across at
least two images.

The integers are absolute over the frozen nine. A quarantined voting owner is
neither material nor nonmaterial and therefore conservatively satisfies no
route.

Permitted reading:

> Mere row insertion, even using a duplicate and sort-inconsistent real row,
> does not reproduce the tail. A `C`-content-specific local interaction
> survives at these frozen boundaries.

This does not identify whether the relevant content is owner identity,
description, novelty, sorted-route consistency, or another joined property.

### `generic_boundary_sensitivity_sufficient_weak_close`

Only after the specificity gate passes, route when `N` is material for at
least `7/9` voting owners across at least three images, including at least
`3/4` clearly separated owners across at least two images.

Permitted reading:

> Generic boundary sensitivity is sufficient; `C`-specific competition is not
> required at these frozen boundaries.

Never say that owner-specific competition was refuted. Duplicate and sorted-
order regression can inflate `N` materiality.

### `inconclusive`

Every other valid pattern stops this line inconclusive. There is no adaptive
threshold, second neutral-row choice, or automatic GPU successor.
