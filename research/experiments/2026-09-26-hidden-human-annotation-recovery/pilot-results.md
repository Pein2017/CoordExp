## Decision and smallest next discriminator

Keep the model/query/evaluation policy frozen. Audit the 32 region-only witnesses
visually against their original-image context and nearby full-query reports,
concealing arm and hidden annotation identities during initial review. The
strongest alternative to new-object discovery is extent variation or assignment
competition among nearby annotations. Two concrete review candidates already
have same-category cross-route prediction overlap around .70 while receiving
different hidden IDs (images16228 and14038); overlap alone proves neither sameness
nor distinctness.

This is an offline complementarity audit nominated using hidden evaluation, not
an operational admission policy or population precision estimate. Preserve
uncertain, instance/group/part and truncated-extent outcomes. It can decide
whether the observed regional complement merits candidate-local verification
design. It cannot itself promote labels or authorize training. The current
CPU-only preparation package is specified once in [unit.md](unit.md).
