## Stage decision and reopening condition

Stop this low-weight52-positive/16-step route after two candidate policies. Do not feed either endpoint into a recursive rollout, select the isolated favorable checkpoint as a winner, increase dose/seeds by default, or promote any labels/checkpoint. This is an evidence-based stop for the tested recipe, not convergence, a general impossibility of self-training, or proof that every unmatched target is wrong.

The useful positive result is a working end-to-end partial-positive training path and proposal support unavailable in ordinary output. The counterexample is that improved opportunity and conditional imitation do not yet deliver durable natural recovery with preservation. Large common invalid-row/visible improvements are primarily shared by supervised replay and corrected losses; their causal components were not isolated. Explicit generated-own-prefix invalid-row negative training remains unimplemented, and this study does not identify the old axis001 loss as the root cause.

Reopen with one new discriminating contract: for example, a separately qualified corrected teacher to test whether cleaner proposals change the support/learning tradeoff, or an objective that tests natural target acquisition against incumbent preservation. A larger unstructured sweep or more repeated queries alone is not justified by these results. Source, raw outputs, candidates, unused dispositions and gained/lost IDs remain reproducible; no model or dataset is promoted.
