# Same-results scientific interpretation benchmark

This brief is frozen before the new crossed outcomes. Root will append one
identical evidence packet (artifact paths and SHA256 bindings) to both candidate
dispatches when the panel passes technical acceptance. No candidate gets the
other answer or private engineering history.

## Question

What does this Source/Rweak64 four-cell intervention actually identify about
annotated-owner changes, and does its strongest result justify a concrete next
learning objective? Treat this as a difficult identifiability and scientific
decision problem, not a request to restate a metric table or propose a model
architecture. The owning `unit.md` fixes semantics, population and stop rule.

For each image, p is the common complete-row prefix, a0/a1 the first divergent
complete Source/Rweak action. Y00/Y11 are original natural diagonals; Y01/Y10
are recipient-recomputed cross continuations after literal p+a1 / p+a0.
Primary evidence is the complete output's category-consistent one-to-one IoU50
annotated-owner set, with burden and stricter IoUs carried alongside. Direct
action changes and matching ambiguity cannot be removed from the primary score.

Your deliverable should:

1. State the exact finite-panel estimands and controlled contrasts supported by
   these four outputs. Explain all binary cross patterns for baseline lost and
   gained owners. Distinguish a measured contrast from a causal mechanism claim.
2. Use the actual evidence to identify the strongest useful complete-output
   result, or conclude that none is established. Report source/donor-relative
   owner gains/losses jointly with prediction burden/F1, not net count alone.
   Identify which results survive conservative direct-row/matching ambiguity
   checks. Do not mislabel a terminal direct B-to-C replacement as suffix failure.
3. Give explicit counterexamples or observationally equivalent mechanisms that
   show the principal non-identifiabilities. Address interaction, greedy-policy
   versus existence of a good suffix, internal state versus visible history, and
   what cannot be inferred about training gradients or independent row versus
   parameter causes. A claim that something is unidentifiable needs a concrete
   construction, not only a cautionary sentence.
4. Decide whether a next learning objective is justified, only as a proposal.
   Specify a minimal objective/contrast and the cheapest falsifying evidence if
   warranted; otherwise state the missing decision-bearing evidence. Distinguish
   improving a complete owner outcome from imitating a convenient trajectory.
   No training, extra GPU study, architecture change or implementation is allowed.
5. Rank at most three plausible alternatives by how much they change the decision
   and the cheapest discriminating evidence. Stop after answering this question.

## Evidence and scope limits

The selection is outcome-stratified and deterministic, not an IID prevalence
sample:16 any-Source-loss /8 gain-only /8 equal-owner-set token-changed cases.
25/32 common prefixes are empty. All64 selected actions are rows; there is no
scientific EOS case. Qualification synthetic EOS evidence is implementation
evidence only. Off-diagonal outputs are conditional interventions, not native
deployment scores, a generalization set, or estimates of population mechanism
frequencies. The full512 original denominator and all exclusions remain visible.
Unmatched predictions are annotation-relative, not automatically hallucinations.
