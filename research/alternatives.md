# Unresolved alternatives and reopening conditions

This is a selective decision-oriented register, not an exhaustive substitute for the [full experiment catalog](experiments/catalog.jsonl) or frozen source corpus. Search both before declaring an idea new. “Not tested” and “technically unanswered” are not negative results. A closed recipe is not a permanent ban on its whole family. None of these entries authorizes execution.

## Current proposed discriminator: distinct-owner progress after recurrence

The [2026-09-17 discussion](experiments/2026-09-17-readout-norm-fresh128/human-review-duplication-discussion.md) prioritizes one bounded inference question: does changing a complete row at naturally reached confirmed recurrence restore later free enumeration without sacrificing credible owners? Compare native continuation, a valid same-owner alternative and an already-supported distinct-owner row under matched conditions, then release original greedy. Exclude the supplied owner from recovery credit; include unlabeled-owner preservation, invalidity and stopping. This is a proposal, not a frozen protocol, executed test or training grant.

The nearest counterexamples are the July22 spatial-history/first-fork distinction and dedup studies that reduced repeats without established owner recovery. A norm-policy pulse followed by withdrawal is a secondary timing contrast, not a substitute for owner-level consequences. Lower overlap can hide jitter, invalidity, legitimate-neighbor suppression or early EOS. Do not turn category concentration, GT ambiguity or boundary coordinates into a proven mechanism. See [history](questions/history-repetition-stopping.md) and [physical evaluation](questions/physical-evaluation.md).

## Already absorbed, not a second backlog

Cumulative fixed-teacher fitting completed through the [22-image stage](experiments/2026-09-15-coco22-cumulative-expansion/results.md); Source256 learning and ranking recipes then closed without joint promotion. Neither outcome settles the entire continuation-learning family. Painted-cue preparation, anti-copy follow-up, stored-delta replay, component/post-scatter localization, residual-boundary decomposition, identity-by-slot checks and canonical-depth probes also have indexed outcomes. The older row-trajectory plan is not evidence of full completion by itself; later four-event sequential control addresses a bounded related scope. Their lessons live in the question pages, with all source records in the catalog. Old “next decider” prose is not sufficient evidence that a task is still unexecuted.

## Before proposing a new unit

Write one short predecessor note: nearest experiment and exact tested conditions; what it actually answered; which idea remains unanswered; what will differ; what possible observation changes the decision. Use the catalog's title/topic and source paths, then read the closest result and protocol. If no suitable predecessor exists, say so. Do not create a ceremonial review chain or a new experiment just to fill a table.
