### Same-prefix mechanism result

The frozen panel has seven deliberately selected exact Source-produced
prefixes and 22 complete candidate rows.

- Continue-minus-stop rises in all seven cases.
- Neither of the two actual Source terminal states flips from stop to continue.
- After the canonical row opener is fixed, best uncovered-minus-covered row
  likelihood improves in five of six comparable cases.
- With a complete one-token description, Source realizes any uncovered owner
  in 4/6 cases and the intended owner in 3/6; transition does so in 3/6 and
  2/6. No covered-owner repeats occur in these releases.
- Supplying only the row opener lets Source recover a missed bottle on held-out
  image 3442. This proves that forced continuation can reveal a true positive,
  but two terminal cases cannot estimate its probability and transition does
  not improve the tested releases.

The bounded disposition is `post-continue-improvement`, qualified by a
concurrent gate shift and no released row-realization advantage. It does not
prove an explicit covered set or objective-recipe causality.

### Complete-row mechanism result

Across 1,440 common exact events, sequence-sum, token-mean, equal-group, and
9/10/11-token-stratum scores retain the same ordering. Sum-versus-mean
normalization does not explain the behavior.

Pairwise and owner-conditioned checkpoints gain owners by 256 tokens, then add
many predictions without more net gain. At 3,084 tokens their prediction counts
and owner yields are `1,396/0.290` and `3,235/0.126`, versus Source
`786/0.495`. Excessive continuation and output expansion are the dominant
full-row failure. Length stops are the worst extremes, not the sole cause.

## Current judgment and strongest uncertainty

Step 36 changes both the decision to continue and relative conditional row
scores. It preserves much better free-rollout efficiency than the full-row
arms, but it does not reliably realize a selected owner after release. The
strongest unresolved alternative is that the selected row-score effect follows
row identity or geometry correlated with owner status rather than a model
computation of which physical owners are already covered.

The current evidence promotes no architecture, explicit state carrier, prompt
change, one-row-at-a-time output contract, production forced-continuation
policy, or final training objective.
