## Decision-relevant evidence

### Continuation locality and exact-prefix owner composition

- Continuation change is broad rather than training-prefix-local. Transition
  step 36 changes opener-minus-terminal by `+1.774` on 1,440 trained exact
  prefixes, `+1.785` on 480 different same-image nonterminal prefixes, and
  `+1.788` on 360 matched untouched nonterminal prefixes. The full-row arms
  show the same near-equality at much larger magnitudes.
- Natural terminal states attenuate but do not remove the shift. Transition
  changes 200 untouched terminal-with-remaining-owner states by `+0.649` on
  average and is positive on 184/200.
- The owner atlas has 400 sampled-reachable prefix-by-owner cases over 311
  images and 382 states. Source intended-owner recovery is `362/400` native,
  `363/400` after forcing only the opener, and `387/400` after forcing the
  complete verified description before geometry release. Transition is
  `369/400`, `369/400`, and `391/400`.
- The sole Source-native terminal case becomes a matched missed `bench` after
  opener forcing; transition natively recovers it. This proves existence, not
  the probability of recovery over actual stops.
- Strict composition support is small: 17 exact states from 16 images yield 38
  ordered owner pairs. Appending one verified owner generally lowers the
  second fixed candidate row's likelihood and continuation margin, yet
  opener-forced second-owner recovery rises from `15/38` to `22/38` for Source
  and from `18/38` to `22/38` for transition, with both gains and losses.
- Atlas admission requires an already observed verified sampled positive row.
  These are conditional recovery rates, not prevalence over every remaining
  annotated owner.

### Robust final-owner result

- Development-256 remains `128 gained / 60 lost / +68 net`.
- Heldout-128 remains `46 gained / 39 lost / +7 net`, with Source/transition
  owners `729/736`, predictions `1,591/1,473`, strict duplicate candidates
  `30/17`, and owner yield `0.4582/0.4997`.
- Held-out net stays positive across generated-token cutoffs, paired natural-
  stop restriction, matching thresholds, and leave-one-image-out analysis.
- Per-image owner-net median is zero. This is a small robustly directional
  aggregate within an analyzed cohort, not population confirmation.
- Blind entity and geometry review preserves the official `+7` ledger and
  yields `22 genuine gains / 16 genuine losses / +6` under the strict human-
  refined rule.

### Same-prefix mechanism result

The frozen panel has seven deliberately selected exact Source-produced
prefixes and 22 complete candidate rows.

- Continue-minus-stop rises in all seven cases.
- Neither of the two actual Source terminal states flips from stop to continue.
- After the canonical row opener is fixed, best uncovered-minus-covered row
  likelihood improves in five of six comparable cases.
- With a complete one-token description, Source realizes any uncovered owner
  in 4/6 cases and the intended owner in 3/6; transition does so in 3/6 and
  2/6. No covered-owner repeats occur in these releases.
- Supplying only the row opener lets Source recover a missed bottle on held-out
  image 3442. This proves that forced continuation can reveal a true positive,
  but two terminal cases cannot estimate its probability and transition does
  not improve the tested releases.

The bounded disposition is `post-continue-improvement`, qualified by a
concurrent gate shift and no released row-realization advantage. It does not
prove an explicit covered set or objective-recipe causality.

### Complete-row mechanism result

Across 1,440 common exact events, sequence-sum, token-mean, equal-group, and
9/10/11-token-stratum scores retain the same ordering. Sum-versus-mean
normalization does not explain the behavior.

Pairwise and owner-conditioned checkpoints gain owners by 256 tokens, then add
many predictions without more net gain. At 3,084 tokens their prediction counts
and owner yields are `1,396/0.290` and `3,235/0.126`, versus Source
`786/0.495`. Excessive continuation and output expansion are the dominant
full-row failure. Length stops are the worst extremes, not the sole cause.

## Current judgment and strongest uncertainty

Step 36 changes both the broad decision to continue and verified owner-row
likelihood. Its continuation effect is not specific to trained exact prefixes.
Within sampled-reachable cases it adds only modest released owner recovery over
Source, with owner churn, while complete-description forcing closes most
remaining failures for both checkpoints. One-step composition preserves
usable second-owner reachability but is order-sensitive and is not monotone in
candidate likelihood.

The strongest immediate uncertainty is the probability that forcing only the
opener after an actual Source stop yields an intended remaining owner, another
uncovered owner, a repeat, or an invalid/unmatched row. The current atlas has
one released Source stop, while 200 additional terminal states were scored but
not released. Arbitrary annotated remaining owners and broader composition
states also remain outside the admitted sampled-positive population.

The current evidence promotes no architecture, explicit state carrier, prompt
change, one-row-at-a-time output contract, production forced-continuation
policy, or final training objective.
