On 2026-07-23 the user corrected the active dense-enumeration treatment route.
The preceding positive complete-row, multiple-route, Source-preservation, and
constant-dose image-breadth screens remain valid diagnostic evidence, but the
training route had drifted toward selecting and imitating one owner or row at a
time. Those objectives can recover the named owner while exchanging away other
useful owners, so they do not directly represent the deployment goal.

The next primary treatment is true order-independent set-level supervision.
For an image and an actual model-produced prefix, every verified uncovered
physical owner is a valid next owner. Candidate continuations must be mapped to
their final unique physical-owner sets. A continuation is preferred only when
its verified owner set strictly contains another continuation's set without
worse confirmed safety failures. Trajectories that gain different owners but
do not contain one another are incomparable and must not be trained as positive
versus negative. Unknown or potentially unlabeled entities receive no negative
gradient.

The first target is a read-only admission census over Source-eligible members
of the frozen training split, followed by a 256-image screen only if the data
contains strict set-dominance edges and multiple valid first-owner aliases.
Unknown-row candidates remain ineligible for the primary preference loss. A
same-prefix valid-versus-harmful row comparison may remain an auxiliary
diagnostic, and token-type gating may remain a stability mechanism, but neither
may collapse the primary objective back to exact cross-entropy on one selected
owner row. Promotion depends on clean greedy gained, retained, and lost-owner
accounting. Object slots, an explicit ledger, an external detector, terminal
suppression, and online refresh remain held unless the set-level loss exposes a
specific need.
