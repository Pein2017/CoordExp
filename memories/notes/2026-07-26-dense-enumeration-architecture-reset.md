## Evidence-backed synthesis

The latest formal owner is:

`research/investigations/qwen3-vl-dense-enumeration/experiments/2026-07-26-source-versus-transition-step36-forced-opener-owner-selection/results.md`

Conceptually separate behavior at a row boundary into continue/stop, owner
selection conditional on continuing, and row realization conditional on the
owner. Existing token-likelihood objectives update all three through one
autoregressive policy. The accumulated evidence shows that continuation is
easy to shift broadly, conditional aggregate strict-owner yield did not improve
under the fixed-opener Source-versus-step-36 comparison, and realization is
usually possible when the owner description is already fixed but remains
vulnerable to geometry drift.

The working diagnosis for external challenge is therefore a missing
permutation-invariant marginal set-completion decision: the policy lacks a
stable interface that compares visually supported owners with already covered
owners, estimates the final-set value of continuing, and selects any uncovered
owner without conflating that decision with row realization. This is a
hypothesis, not a promoted mechanism or architecture. The strongest alternative
is that a set-valued or final-set objective on the existing transformer is
sufficient and no explicit set-difference module is necessary.
