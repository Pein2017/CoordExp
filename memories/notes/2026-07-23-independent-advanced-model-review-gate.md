# Independent Advanced-Model Review Gate

On 2026-07-23, the user authorized outside independent review at important or
stage-defining research difficulties. The reviewer is provider-independent: a
Pro model, Fable model, or another advanced model may serve, provided it is
outside the primary implementation and internal-audit chain.

The review is not a substitute for a small decisive experiment. It is most
useful when competing explanations survive focused evidence, or when a
mathematical, causal, or statistical assumption controls an expensive training
launch, architecture choice, scale-up, or claim boundary. Reviewers receive the
same frozen decision packet independently and do not see one another's
answers. The main research lead reconciles their counterexamples and advice
with executable evidence and retains final decision authority.

For the current dense-enumeration route, the admission census remains an
internal empirical task. If the census leaves the set-level trajectory score,
owner-preservation semantics, matched control, or launch interpretation
materially ambiguous, an independent advanced-model review should occur after
the census and before the 256-image training unit is frozen.
